# What changed since the submission (16 Oct → 30 Oct 2026)

Two changes are in the live demo. Both came from testing the system on new cases written by a
separate model that never saw the code (a "blind set"), and both make the system hand more of the
right cases to a person and pay the right party. Every step, with the cases and figures, is in
[08_DESIGN_DECISIONS.md](08_DESIGN_DECISIONS.md) (entries D66 to D66e and D68).

## 1. Harassment now always reaches a person (Responsible AI)

**Why.** Ryde's policy v2.1 s5.2 says a safety incident, including harassment or unwanted personal
or romantic advances, is prepared by AI but decided by a person. In a test on 29 Oct, a rider
complained that the driver stopped short, but also that he asked about her boyfriend, commented on
her looks and messaged her after the trip. The system ruled on the drop-off itself, twice, and the
safety part was lost.

**What changed.** We added three layers, and each one only ever sends a case to a person; none of
them changes an amount:
- **A phrase list.** Code checks the complaint and the other party's chat messages for clear phrases
  such as "commenting on my looks", "asked me out" or "you look really nice tonight, can I have your
  number". Phrases with an innocent reason are left out, such as "your number to return the wallet".
- **A model check on the chat.** It looks for an unwanted advance or touch by the *other* party: a
  remark on looks or body, pressing for a number, address or meeting, waiting outside the person's
  home, or a touch that was not needed. The model must quote the exact words, and code checks the
  quote against the chat or the complaint, so the model cannot invent an incident.
- **The same check on the complaint alone**, for cases with no chat at all.

**Result, on three new blind sets (one run per case).**

| Test | Harassment cases sent to a person | Look-alike cases decided correctly on the numbers |
|---|---|---|
| Before the change (the 29 Oct case, 2 runs) | 0 of 1 (ruled by AI both times) | – |
| Phrase list only (set HR) | 4 of 6, only 2 through the safety route | – |
| With the chat check (set HS) | **6 of 6** | **4 of 4** |
| No chat at all (set HN) | **6 of 6**, all through the safety route | 4 of 4 after one fix* |

\* In set HN, the phrase list wrongly flagged "he asked for my number to arrange the return of my
wallet". The phrase list no longer matches a number asked for a reason; the model check decides
those cases instead. In a scan of all 256 test cases, it now flags only real harassment cases.

Look-alikes that are **not** sent to a person include:
- tapping a sleeping rider on the shoulder at the destination;
- helping an elderly passenger out of the car;
- asking a rider to sit in front because a child seat is fitted;
- an angry rider with no advance.

## 2. A driver who wins a claim now gets paid

**Why.** When a driver filed for money, the Judge often reasoned correctly but wrote the award as
text ("S$5.00 released to driver") instead of in the amount field. This happened with an extra-stop
fee, a held cancellation fee and a cleaning fee. The payout step reads only that field, so the driver
received nothing. In one case, the Judge listed the rider's wish not to pay as a second request and
labelled the ruling "partly upheld, S$0".

**What changed.**
- **The Judge's instructions** now say:
  - the amount field holds the money the person who filed receives, and that includes a driver;
  - only the filer's own requests are listed;
  - when the rules give less than was asked, the request is "partly" granted for the rule amount, not
    refused.
- **A new code check** sends a ruling back to the Judge once when it grants a driver's money request
  but leaves the amount empty. If it is still empty, a person decides. Code never fills in the amount
  itself: every amount still comes from the Judge's own arithmetic on record figures, which is checked.
- **The payout record** now says "pay the driver", not "refund the passenger".

**Result.**
- **Earlier rulings.** On the 83 saved rulings, the new check flagged 6, and all 6 were real cases of
  "granted, but nothing paid". Rerun after the fix, all five cases behind them pay the right amount.
- **New blind set (DA, 8 cases).** 6 of 8 are fully right:
  - 3 driver awards: S$6.00, S$8.00 and S$4.00;
  - 2 refusals;
  - 1 rider refund of S$2.50.
- **DA-06.** It pays the right amount (nothing, because the fee had already been credited) under a
  different label.
- **DA-04.** The driver asked for S$10 and the rules give S$4.50. It now computes S$4.50 but goes to a
  person, because our arithmetic check accepts only figures that appear in the records, and the
  14-minute wait was worked out from two times.

## Still open (honest list)

- Tests are one run per case, on small sets of 8 to 10 cases.
- When the Judge rejects a driver's own claim, it sometimes also issues the driver a warning. The
  Fairness check then sends the case to a person, which is safe but unnecessary.
- **Learning loop v2** is being built and tested separately and is **not** in the live demo. It
  covers:
  - plain-language precedents from reviewers;
  - matching on the deciding facts;
  - a release gate that blocks a precedent if the test results get worse.
