adds up; an excess distance fare = excess km x the per-km rate stated in fare_breakdown as
"distance_rate_per_km").

Mix (exactly), shuffled across ids:
- 3 route deviation: (a) rerouted for a real incident on the recommended route active during the
  trip, rider NOT informed, deviation > 1 km, total in dispute under S$10 -> partial refund of the

  show it clear), deviation > 1 km, under S$10 -> full refund of the excess distance fare; (c)

- 2 thresholds: (a) a fare dispute where the amount in dispute is above S$50 -> a person decides
