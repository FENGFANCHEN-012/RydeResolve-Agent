Cancellation-scope test set CS-01 .. CS-10 (2026-10-14). Purpose: test who a no-show / cancellation
fee covers when the DRIVER is the one who presses cancel.

Every case is a no_show or cancellation dispute in Singapore, October 2026 (+08:00). Each case has a
"cancellation_policy" block in the {"value": ..., "text": ...} format of the examples, with these keys
and EXACTLY these texts (the values may differ per case):
- free_wait_time_min: "Minutes the driver must wait at the pickup point, counted from arrival, before
  any waiting or cancellation fee may apply to the rider."
- no_show_threshold_min: "Minutes the driver must wait at the pickup point, counted from arrival,
  before the rider counts as a no-show."
- cancellation_fee_after_wait: "The fee in SGD charged to the rider when a booking is cancelled after
  the driver has waited past the free waiting time. It covers rider cancellations and rider no-shows,
  including the no-show cancellation the driver presses after waiting past the threshold; it does not
  cover a cancellation the driver makes for the driver's own reasons."
- fee_goes_to: "Says who receives a cancellation or no-show fee once it is charged. It governs where
  the money goes, not whether the fee is due."
Trip records must have driver_arrival_time (or null), cancellation_time, cancellation_fee and
cancellation_reason; app_events must include driver_arrived (when the driver arrived) and the
cancellation event naming who pressed cancel.

Mix (exactly), shuffled across ids:
- 4 cases where the driver arrived (driver_arrived event, GPS at the pickup), waited past the
  no-show threshold, and the DRIVER pressed cancel as a rider no-show: the fee is due. 2 filed by the
  rider asking for a refund (dismissed, refund 0), 2 filed by the driver asking for a held fee to be
  released (upheld, amount = the fee). In at least two the rider's description stresses that "the
  driver cancelled, not me".
- 2 cases where the driver pressed cancel BEFORE the threshold was reached (the wait is short by a
  clear margin, at least 1 minute): no fee is due, the rider gets the fee back (upheld, refund = fee).
- 2 cases where the driver cancelled for the driver's own reasons (e.g. the driver says in chat the
  car broke down, or the driver chose to take another job) even though the cancel reason field says
  rider_no_show: the fee is not due, refund the rider (upheld, refund = fee). The driver's own chat
  message must make the reason clear.
- 1 case where the RIDER pressed cancel after the driver had waited past the free waiting time: the
  fee is due (rider dispute dismissed, refund 0).
- 1 case where the records conflict on whether the driver was at the pickup (driver_arrived event but
  GPS 900 m away at that time): a person decides (verdict null, requires_human_review true,
  must_escalate true).
Do not make the answer obvious from the title or the description.

Every case: expected_outcome with verdict, refund_amount, recommended_action, requires_human_review,
must_escalate, reason (cite the exact records, times and figures, and the wait in minutes),
expected_dispute_type, acceptable_verdicts where honest, heldout: true. Dispute ids CS-01..CS-10,
trip ids RYDE-CS-001.. . For a driver's claim upheld, refund_amount is the amount released to the
driver.
