Precedent experiment (2026-10-13). Two sets written together by one blind author:

A. Training set PT-01 .. PT-10 ("past cases"). These become precedents after a human reviewer
   decides them. Every one is a grey-area case: a cautious automated system would likely hand it
   to a person, yet an experienced reviewer who reads the records and the case's rules carefully
   can decide it without more information. Use a different source of doubt in each, for example:
   - a discretion rule (refund_at_ryde_discretion or similar) where the records point clearly
     one way;
   - a doubtful record (GPS dropout, telemetry gap, sync error) that is beside the decisive
     point, or whose point another clean record settles;
   - a time rule at or near its boundary, with the rule text saying whether the limit counts;
   - a strong side issue the filer leans on that the case's rules make irrelevant;
   - a goodwill situation (first complaint, small amount, clear service failure) under a
     discretion rule;
   - two records that look contradictory but are not once units or time zones are read right.
   No safety, harassment or fraud allegation in the training set.

B. Test set PX-01 .. PX-16 ("new cases"). Held out; nobody tunes on them.
   - PX-01 .. PX-10: each is a sibling of one training case (name it in "_sibling_of"): the same
     kind of doubt in a different place, with different people, times and amounts. In 7 of them
     the right outcome follows the same principle as the sibling. In 3 of them one fact differs
     so that the sibling's principle does NOT apply and the outcome changes (for example the
     doubtful record is now on the decisive point, the time is now past the limit, or the
     discretion rule is absent). Do not make the difference obvious from the title.
   - PX-11 .. PX-16: controls that look similar to a training case on the surface but must go to
     a person (verdict null, requires_human_review true, must_escalate true): two with a doubtful
     record on the decisive point and no clean record settling it, one fraud signal, one safety or
     harassment allegation, one where the case's own rules say a person decides, one where two
     clean records truly contradict each other on the decisive point.

Dispute types: spread over no_show, cancellation_refund, fare_dispute, route_deviation,
service_quality, cleaning_fee, driver_rights. At least 3 training and 3 test cases filed by the
driver. Use the same policy key names the example cases use, with a value and a text that says
exactly what is measured and from which moment. All timestamps for one moment must agree across
trip_data, gps_telemetry and app_events unless the case is meant to contain a conflict.

Every case (both sets): expected_outcome with verdict, refund_amount, recommended_action,
requires_human_review, must_escalate, reason (cite the exact records and figures),
expected_dispute_type, acceptable_verdicts where the rules honestly allow more than one label,
and heldout: true. Amounts in SGD, Singapore places, dates in October 2026, times +08:00.
Dispute ids PT-01.. / PX-01..; trip ids RYDE-PT-001.. / RYDE-PX-001...
