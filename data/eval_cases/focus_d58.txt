Blind set D58 (D58-01 .. D58-08). Purpose: check two changes on cases nobody has tuned against:
rules that count FULL units (partial minutes or km dropped) or cap the credit, and rulings that must
not rest on facts nobody needs.

Mix (exactly):
- 3 cases refunded in part (verdict partially_upheld) under a per-unit rule in the trip's rules that
  counts only FULL minutes or FULL km (say so in the rule text: "partial minutes are dropped"), where
  the measured quantity is fractional (e.g. a stop of 10 min 40 s, an arrival 7 min 50 s past the
  ETA, a pickup of 5.6 km). Different units and rates. At least one filed by the driver. Correct
  result: an automatic ruling with that exact amount.
- 1 case refunded in part where the per-unit credit would exceed the rule's cap, so the cap is paid
  (e.g. capped at 30% of the fare). Correct result: automatic, the cap amount.
- 1 case where the rule counts every STARTED minute ("a partial minute counts as a full minute"),
  the quantity is fractional, and the refund is computed rounding UP. Correct result: automatic with
  the rounded-up amount (state it in the key; acceptable_verdicts partially_upheld only).
- 1 route-deviation case where the detour followed a road closure recorded by the platform (a
  traffic or reroute event), the driver told the rider in chat before turning, the rider agreed, and
  the extra distance matches the recorded reroute. The rider still complains about the higher
  charge. Correct result: dismissed, refund 0, automatic.
- 1 route-deviation case where the trip's rule requires the driver to notify the rider BEFORE a
  deviation, the driver deviated without any message or call before it (the first chat message comes
  after), there was no closure, and the rule refunds the excess distance fare. Correct result:
  automatic refund of that excess, computed from the records.
- 1 case where the correct result is a person (verdict null, requires_human_review true,
  must_escalate true): a FULL-minute credit rule, but two records disagree on the decisive time (e.g.
  the resume event and the GPS show different times by several minutes), and nothing settles which
  is right.
