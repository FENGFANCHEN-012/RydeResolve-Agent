Fresh test set (FS-01 .. FS-20). Purpose: a clean measurement on cases nobody has tuned against.
It covers the whole job, not one weakness: charges that stand, charges refunded in full or in
part, and cases a person must decide.

Mix (exactly):
- 6 cases where the charge stands in full (verdict dismissed, refund 0).
- 5 cases refunded in full (verdict upheld).
- 4 cases refunded in part (verdict partially_upheld) where the records let a reviewer compute
  the figure (for example one allowed charge and one not, or a fee reduced to what the case's
  rule allows).
- 5 cases where the correct result is a person (verdict null, requires_human_review true,
  must_escalate true), each for a different reason: two records that contradict each other on
  the decisive point, a record the decision needs is missing or broken, a fraud signal, a safety
  or harassment allegation, and a dispute the case's own rules say needs a person.

Dispute types: cycle no_show, cancellation_refund, fare_dispute, route_deviation,
service_quality, cleaning_fee, driver_rights. At least 3 cases filed by the driver.

Time rules: at least 8 cases turn on a time rule in the case's own policy block (no-show wait,
free cancellation window after matching, driver delay past the ETA shown at assignment, an
advance booking cancelled some minutes before the scheduled time, a waiting-time threshold).
Use the same policy key names the example cases use, with a value and a text that says exactly
what is measured and from which moment. Include boundary cases: a measure exactly at the limit
(say in the rule text whether the limit counts), a few seconds either side of it, and one where
the filer's own account of the times is wrong but the platform records are clear. All
timestamps for one moment must agree across trip_data, gps_telemetry and app_events unless the
case is meant to contain a conflict.

Side issues: about half the cases carry a side issue the filer leans on that does not change the
outcome under the case's rules (a curt message, a short delay, an app glitch afterwards, the
filer was upset). In a few, the case's rules make that issue decisive. Include
refund_at_ryde_discretion or a similar discretion rule in some policy blocks, not all.

Absences: in some cases the answer depends on something not being in a platform record (no
driver_arrived event, no photo attached, a null field). In one, a party claims the other "never
said" something in the chat, while the chat log is plainly incomplete (they also spoke by phone).

Every case: expected_outcome with verdict, refund_amount, recommended_action,
requires_human_review, must_escalate, reason (cite the exact records and figures),
expected_dispute_type, and acceptable_verdicts where the rules honestly allow more than one
label. Amounts in SGD, Singapore places, dates in October 2026, times +08:00.
