Ryde Refund Policy:
- Full refund if driver cancels the ride
- 50% refund if passenger cancels after 2 minutes
- No charge if passenger cancels within 2 minutes

Ryde Driver Code of Conduct:
- Drivers must arrive within 10 minutes of acceptance
- Drivers must not ask passengers to cancel
- Drivers must maintain a 4.5+ rating
- Three violations may result in account suspension

Ryde Safety Guidelines:
- All rides are GPS tracked
- Passengers can share trip details with emergency contacts
- SOS button available in-app
