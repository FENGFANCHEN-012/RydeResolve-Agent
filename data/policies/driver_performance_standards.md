**Publisher:** Ryde Technologies Pte. Ltd.

## 1. Overview

Ryde maintains high service standards for its driver partners. This document outlines the performance metrics, community standards, and consequences for failing to meet these standards. The Driver Rating system is in place to maintain high Driver-Partner quality.

## 2. Driver Performance Metrics

### 2.1 Driver Rating

- Drivers are rated by riders on a scale of 1-5 stars after each trip.
- The driver's overall rating is an average of all ratings received.
- **Threshold:** A driver's account may be placed on hold if their rating falls below the service standard.
- Upon reinstatement after suspension, ratings are reset to 4.90.

### 2.2 Cancel/Completion Rate (CR)

- The CR metric tracks the ratio of cancelled trips to completed trips.
- High cancellation rates negatively impact the user experience and the Ryde platform.
- Upon reinstatement after suspension, CR is reset to 0%.

### 2.3 Acceptance Rate

- Drivers should only accept bookings they intend to fulfill.
- Accepting and then cancelling repeatedly is considered "acceptance abuse."
- A driver's acceptance rate is tracked and displayed in their profile metrics.

### 2.4 Unique Trip Count

- Ryde tracks the number of unique trips completed by each driver.
- This metric is used to assess driver experience and reliability.

## 3. Service Standards

### 3.1 What Constitutes Failing Service Standards

A driver may be placed on temporary hold (suspension) for:

- Consistently low ratings (below the service standard threshold).
- High cancellation rate without acceptable reasons.
- Repeated unjustified cancellations (3+ in a 7-day period may trigger account review).
- Safety-related incidents.
- Unprofessional conduct toward riders.
- Non-compliance with platform rules and regulations.

### 3.2 What Constitutes Violating Community Standards

A driver may face permanent deactivation for:

- Physical or verbal assault or harassment.
- Discrimination based on race, religion, gender, or other protected characteristics.
- Contacting passengers after the trip has ended.
- Using someone else's account or sharing account credentials.
- Engaging in off-platform jobs that bypass the Ryde platform system.
- Operating without valid registrations or permits.
- Promoting or participating in unlicensed or unauthorized ride-hailing.
- Fraudulent activity, including fake-complete trips.
- Engaging in conduct that could damage Ryde's reputation.

## 4. Cancellation Standards

### 4.1 Acceptable Cancellation Reasons

Cancellations must be based on acceptable reasons shown in the Application:

- Rider is not at the pickup location after the grace period (3 minutes).
- Rider behaves inappropriately or is a safety concern.
- Rider requests an illegal or unsafe destination.
- Vehicle breakdown or medical emergency (documentation required).
- Rider booked the wrong service or wrong location and refused to cancel.
- Rider came with additional riders beyond what was indicated.
- Rider arrived with a child without a booster seat.

### 4.2 Unacceptable Cancellations

- High and/or frequent cancellation rates impair the User experience.
- Unacceptable cancellations or ignoring bookings may result in temporary suspension.
- Cancellations without valid reason after acceptance impact acceptance rate and rating.

## 5. Account Suspension and Reinstatement

### 5.1 Reasons for Account Disabling

- **Failing Service Standards:** Leads to a temporary hold (suspension/disabling) on the account.
- **Community Standards Violations:** Repeated failure to abide by community standards leads to permanent removal (permanent deactivation) from the platform.

### 5.2 The Reinstatement Process

1. **Check Your Email:** Review your Ryde-registered email for specific details regarding the temporary hold on your account.
2. **Submit an Appeal:** If you believe the account disability is not justified, submit an appeal via email to support@rydesharing.com.
3. **Review Timeline:** Ryde states they will assess the case promptly and strive to resolve it within 3 days.
4. **Appeal Approved:** If the appeal is approved, the account is restored. A processing fee of S$10.00 (excluding GST) is applied for account restoration. If the appeal is approved, this fee is waived.
5. **Account Reset:** Upon reinstatement, ratings are reset to 4.90 and CR (Cancel/Completion Rate) is reset to 0%.

### 5.3 Processing Fee for Restoration

- **Amount:** S$10.00 (excluding GST).
- **Purpose:** Covers operational costs associated with account restoration:
  - Re-verifying your profile and backend processing (including document review).
  - Resetting your rating and review to offer a clean slate.
  - Maintaining a fair and secure environment for all users.
- **Waiver:** If the appeal is approved, the fee is waived.

### 5.4 Cashout Assistance

- If the account is temporarily on hold, the driver may write to support to request assistance with cashing out their funds.
- Instant cashout may be disabled or suspended for accounts with issues.

## 6. 0% Commission Model

### 6.1 How It Works

- Drivers take home 100% of their earnings.
- No commission is deducted from trip fares.
- Drivers receive the full fare, minus applicable platform service fees and government-mandated taxes (GST).

### 6.2 Instant Cashout

- Drivers can get paid anytime, anywhere.
- Instant cashout allows immediate access to earnings.
- May be disabled or suspended for accounts with issues.

### 6.3 Standard Cashout

- Alternative to instant cashout.
- Standard processing time applies.

## 7. New Scheme for Taxi and Private Hire Car Drivers

## 8. Ryde's Commitment to Driver-Partners

Ryde commits to:

- 0% Commission: Drivers take home all their earnings.
- 100% Support: Providing support every step of the way.
- Maximum Flexibility: Drivers select trips based on when and where they want to drive.
- Instant Cashout: Get paid anytime, anywhere.
- High driver community standards and comprehensive review systems.
