# Ryde Privacy Policy

**Source:** https://rydesharing.com/privacy-policy/
**Publisher:** Ryde Technologies Pte. Ltd.
**Contact:** dataprotection@rydesharing.com

## 1. Overview

This Privacy Policy governs how Ryde Technologies Pte Ltd ("Ryde") collects, uses, processes, and discloses Personal Data through its mobile applications, websites, and related services globally. The policy applies to passengers, driver partners, contractors, and service providers.

**Definition of Personal Data:** Any information that can identify you, including but not limited to name, nationality, telephone number, bank/credit card details, email address, image, government-issued ID numbers, biometric data, race, date of birth, marital status, religion, health information, vehicle and insurance information, employment, and financial information.

## 2. Collection of Personal Data

### 2.1 Voluntarily Provided

Ryde collects data when users:

- Complete registration forms or user profiles.
- Provide driver eligibility information (license, vehicle, background checks).
- Interact with social media pages.
- Participate in contests or events.
- Verify identity (social media logins, selfies, payment card verification).
- Fill out demographic surveys.
- Agree to in-vehicle audio/video recording during rides.

### 2.2 Automatically Collected

- **Location data** (pick-up locations, route variations, GPS tracking).
- Feedback, ratings, and compliments.
- Transaction information (payment method, distance travelled).
- Usage interaction data (features used, content viewed).
- Device information (hardware model, IP address, advertising identifiers).
- In-app chat message content.
- Audio/video recordings and related metadata during rides.

### 2.3 From Other Sources

- Referral programs.
- Business partners (fleet partners, payment providers, ride-hailing partners).
- Insurance and financial providers.
- Publicly available data sources.
- Governmental data sources.
- Emergency contacts added by users.
- In-app chat usage.
- Marketing services providers.

### 2.4 Sensitive Personal Data

Includes race, national ID information, religious beliefs, background information (financial/criminal records where legally permissible), health data, disability, marital status, and biometric data. Collected only with consent and in strict compliance with applicable laws.

### 2.5 In-Vehicle Recording

- Ryde may install in-vehicle audio/video recording devices for safety and security.
- Personal in-vehicle cameras installed by partners are the partner's responsibility.
- Recordings may be used for safety monitoring, detecting unsafe driving behavior, fraud prevention, and crime prosecution.

## 4. Disclosure of Personal Data

Ryde shares Personal Data with:

1. **Other Users:** Pick-up/drop-off locations, ratings, driver name/photo, vehicle details (with passengers); recipient/delivery partner details (for RydeSEND); mobile number and Ryde-registered name (for in-app chat).
2. **Ryde Partners:** At user request (e.g., promotion providers, vehicle service partners).
3. **Corporate Account Owners:** Trip data when using employer's Ryde corporate account.
4. **Subsidiaries and Affiliates:** Associated companies and jointly controlled entities.
5. **Service Providers/Business Partners:** Payment processors, cloud storage providers, marketing partners, data analytics providers, research partners, insurance/financing partners.
6. **Legal Advisors and Government Authorities:** For legal purposes, emergencies threatening life/health/safety, or public interest.

## 5. Retention of Personal Data

- Retained only for the period necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined.
- Longer retention only if required/allowed by law.
- Once no longer necessary, data is erased, destroyed, anonymised, or access is prevented.
- May retain data for safety, security, fraud prevention/detection per applicable laws.

## 6. Data Protection Measures

- Takes reasonable legal, organizational, and technical measures.
- Access limited to employees on a need-to-know basis.
- Authorized processing with confidentiality requirements.
- Internet transmission is not completely secure; transmission remains at user's own risk.

## 7. User Rights

Users may be entitled to:

- Ask about processing and receive a copy of their Personal Data.
- Request correction and/or deletion of Personal Data.
- Request restriction of processing or object to processing.
- Withdraw consent (where processing is consent-based).
- Request data portability (machine-readable format).
- Complain to relevant data privacy authority.

**Important:** Withdrawing consent or not sharing data may result in inability to use Services. Ryde screens and verifies all requests before action. Supporting documentation may be required.

## 8. Cookies and Third-Party Advertising

- Uses cookies, web beacons, tags, scripts, and advertising identifiers.
- Third parties may use cookies on Ryde's platforms.
- Shares non-personally identifiable data with third parties for targeted advertising.
- **User Control:** Disable cookies via browser settings; limit sharing via App Settings > Privacy > Ads and mobile device settings.

## 9. Ryde Corporate Account

### 9.1 Data Controller Relationship

- Ryde acts as an independent data controller (not a data processor for the Client).
- Client (employer) also acts as an independent data controller.
- Each party individually determines purposes and means of processing.

### 9.2 Data Disclosed to Clients

- Trip and booking information tagged as business purposes (for Corporate Billing).
- No other Personal Data disclosed to Client.

Ryde reserves the right to modify, update, or amend this Policy at any time by posting the updated version on the Websites. Continued use of Services after modifications signifies acceptance.
