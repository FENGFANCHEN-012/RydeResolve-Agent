# Ryde Ratings and Reviews Moderation Policy

**Source:** Ryde Help Center — Ratings and Reviews Moderation Policy
**URL:** https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/20244161586585-Ratings-and-Reviews-Moderation-Policy
**Publisher:** Ryde Technologies Pte. Ltd.

## 1. Overview

At Ryde, the Driver Rating system is in place to maintain high Driver-Partner quality. This policy outlines circumstances where unfair ratings and reviews given by riders may be removed and ratings reinstated.

## 2. Criteria for Review Removal and Rating Reinstatement

A review may be removed and ratings reinstated if:

1. The review is in violation of Ryde's Terms of Use.
2. The review is biased or fraudulent.
3. The review is irrelevant to the rider's Point-to-Point transport experience.

## 3. Conflict of Interest Policy (Fake/Inflated Reviews)

### 3.1 Prohibited Actions

Ryde will remove reviews where there are signals that:

- The trip request was made for the sole purpose of inflating a person's overall rating.
- They suspect the trip never occurred (i.e., a fake-complete trip).
- Accepting fake requests in exchange for a positive review.
- Using a second account to review your own driving performance.
- Providing something of value in exchange for positive reviews.

### 3.2 Penalties for Violations

Violations may result in:

- Restriction of the Ryde driver account.
- Suspension of the Ryde driver account.
- Termination of the Ryde driver account.

## 4. Moderating for Relevance

Reviews and derived ratings that are irrelevant to a driver's performance can be distracting and impact the driver's overall rating. Rider reviews should focus on the driving service during the trip experience.

### 4.1 Content That Should Be Avoided

The following content is not relevant and may be removed:

1. Commentary about a person's social, political, or religious views.
2. Profanity, name-calling, and assumptions about a person's character or personality.
3. Content that refers to circumstances entirely outside of the driver's control (e.g., Mapping / GPS / Product / Technical Issues).
4. Content about services not related to Ryde (e.g., Ryde's competitors).
5. Commentary about past trips/drivers or about the Ryde product where it does not relate to the driver and the specific trip being rated (e.g., Cashbacks, app UI/UX, Waiting Time Fee, etc.).

## 5. Dispute Handling and Rider Penalties

### 5.1 Review Removal Process

- When Ryde receives a report of a review that violates this policy, they may remove the review from the platform.
- The driver's rating may be reinstated to 5 stars for that specific trip.

### 5.2 Repeated Violations

- Repeated violations by the account responsible for the reviews may lead to:
  - Suspension of the rider's account.
  - Permanent deactivation of the rider's account.

## 6. Rating Retaliation

While not explicitly labeled as "Rating Retaliation Policy," the conflict of interest and penalty sections cover:

- Prohibitions against exchanging value for reviews.
- Consequences for fake or biased reviews.
- Patterns of retaliatory ratings (giving unfairly low ratings in response to receiving one) may result in account review.

## 7. Low Rating Impact

### 7.1 For Drivers

- Drivers with an average rating below the service standard threshold may be placed on temporary hold.
- Upon reinstatement, ratings are reset to 4.90.

### 7.2 For Riders

- Riders with consistently low ratings may have their booking priority reduced.
- Repeated low-rated behavior may lead to account review.

## 8. How to Report an Unfair Review

Drivers who believe a review violates this policy can:

1. Report the review through the Ryde Driver App via the RydeHELP feature.
2. Provide details on why the review should be removed (violation of Terms, biased/fraudulent, irrelevant).
3. Ryde will review the report and determine if the review should be removed.
4. If approved, the review is removed and the driver's rating is reinstated.
