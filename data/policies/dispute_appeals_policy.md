# Ryde Dispute Appeals Policy

**Source:** Ryde Help Center — I wish to make an appeal
**URL:** https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/20244103989529-I-wish-to-make-an-appeal
**Publisher:** Ryde Technologies Pte. Ltd.

## 1. Overview

This policy outlines the appeals process for drivers on the Ryde platform. Drivers can submit appeals through the RydeHELP feature in the Ryde driver app for various types of disputes and claims.

## 2. How to File an Appeal

Drivers must submit appeals through the **RydeHELP feature** in the Ryde driver app. Required documentation varies by appeal type (detailed below).

## 3. Appeal Categories

### 3.1 Cancellation/Waiting Fee Appeals

Drivers can appeal for cancellation or waiting fees under the following circumstances:

1. Rider booked the wrong service or wrong location and refused to cancel.
2. Rider cancelled the trip once the driver reached the pickup point.
3. Rider came with additional riders.
4. Rider arrived with a child without a booster seat.
5. RydeSEND order was delayed for more than 3 minutes and no waiting fee was received.
6. RydeSEND order was cancelled after 3 minutes and no cancellation fee was received.
7. Driver cancelled a RydeSEND trip because the order wasn't ready after 3 minutes but no cancellation fee was received.

**Eligibility Criteria:** To qualify for a cancellation/waiting fee, the driver must have arrived at the pickup location and the customer was not present within 3 minutes of arrival. If a customer cancels, the fee is automatically credited if the driver meets eligibility.

### 3.2 ERP (Electronic Road Pricing) Charge Claims

Drivers who forgot to include ERP charges in fares can file a claim with:

- Name of ERP Gantry
- Time of Entry
- Screenshot of Cashcard Statement

**Important:** RydePOOL, RydeHIRE, and RydeSEND trips already include ERP — no separate claim is needed for these services.

### 3.3 Vehicle Mess/Cleaning Damage Claims

Drivers can appeal for cleaning compensation for:

1. Vomit
2. Seat stains
3. Spillage
4. Soil

**Required evidence:**

- Photo evidence (e.g., a picture of the vomit/stain with timestamp).
- Receipt from the Cleaning Company including:
  - Name, contact number & address of the cleaning company.
  - Date of cleaning.
  - Authorised personnel's signature/company stamp.

**Tips:**

- Keep plastic bags handy for nauseous passengers.
- Use newspapers to protect seats when transporting pets.

### 3.4 Account Suspension Appeals

If a driver's account has been disabled, they can appeal:

1. Check your Ryde-registered email for specific details regarding the temporary hold.
2. Submit an appeal via email to support@rydesharing.com.
3. Ryde will assess the case promptly and strive to resolve it within 3 days.
4. If the appeal is approved, the account is restored.
5. A processing fee of S$10.00 (excluding GST) is applied for account restoration. If the appeal is approved, this fee is waived.
6. Upon reinstatement, ratings are reset to 4.90 and CR (Cancel/Completion Rate) is reset to 0%.

## 4. Rider Dispute Process

### 4.1 Filing a Dispute

If a user feels they were incorrectly charged, they may contact support@rydesharing.com. The dispute should include:

- Booking/order ID
- Reason for disputing the charge
- Any supporting evidence (chat logs, location data, timestamps)

### 4.2 Valid Dispute Grounds for No-Show Charges

| Ground | Description | Required Evidence |
|--------|-------------|-------------------|
| Rider present at pickup | Rider was at the location within grace period | GPS location, timestamp |
| Wrong pickup location | Driver went to wrong address | GPS trace of driver vs. pickup point |
| Premature no-show marking | Driver marked no-show before grace period expired | Timestamp comparison |
| No contact from driver | Rider waited and driver did not communicate | Call log, chat log |
| Driver significantly delayed | Driver was more than 10 minutes beyond estimated arrival | Timestamp, GPS trace |

### 4.3 Resolution Outcomes

| Outcome | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Full refund | Cancellation fee fully refunded to rider |
| Partial refund | Portion of fee refunded |
| Charge upheld | Cancellation fee is valid; no refund issued |
| Driver penalty | Driver's behavior flagged; repeated incidents may lead to account review |

## 5. Legal Dispute Resolution

### 5.1 Governing Law

These Terms of Service are governed by Singapore law, without regard to choice or conflicts of law provisions of any jurisdiction.

### 5.2 Arbitration

Any disputes, actions, claims, or causes of action arising out of or in connection with the Terms or the Service shall be referred to the Singapore International Arbitration Centre (SIAC), in accordance with SIAC Rules, by a sole arbitrator. If parties cannot agree, the arbitrator is appointed by the President of SIAC.

- The seat and venue of arbitration is Singapore.
- The language is English.
- Arbitrator fees are borne equally, though the arbitrator may determine otherwise.

### 5.3 Partner Mediation Option

Notwithstanding the arbitration clause, if you are a Partner, you may choose to submit a Dispute for mediation at:

- The Singapore Mediation Centre.
- The Small Claims Tribunal.

Subject to their rules and guidelines.
