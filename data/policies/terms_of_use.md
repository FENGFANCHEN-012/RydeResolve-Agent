## 2. Rider Obligations

### 2.1 Accurate Information
Riders must provide accurate pickup and dropoff locations when booking a ride. Providing misleading location information to manipulate pricing or driver assignment constitutes a violation of these Terms.

### 2.2 Punctuality
- Riders must be at the designated pickup location within **5 minutes** of the driver's arrival for standard bookings.
- For Ryde+ subscribers, a grace period of **10 minutes** applies.
- If the rider is not present at the pickup location within the grace period, the driver may mark the rider as a "no-show" and a cancellation fee may apply.

### 2.3 Conduct During Ride
Riders must:
- Refrain from eating, drinking, or smoking in the vehicle (unless the driver explicitly permits)
- Wear seatbelts at all times
- Treat the driver with respect and courtesy
- Not ask the driver to violate traffic laws
- Not engage in behavior that compromises the safety of the driver, other passengers, or the public

### 2.4 Payment
Riders are responsible for paying the full fare displayed at the end of the trip, including surge pricing if applicable. Payment must be made through the Ryde platform (cashless payment methods).

## 3. Driver Obligations

### 3.1 Route Compliance
Drivers must:
- Follow the most efficient route to the destination as determined by the in-app navigation system, unless the rider requests a specific route
- Not intentionally take longer routes to increase the fare
- Inform the rider if a route change is necessary due to road closures, traffic, or safety concerns

**Route Deviation Policy**: If a driver takes a route that is more than **10% longer** than the optimal route without reasonable justification (traffic, road closures, rider request), the rider may dispute the fare and be eligible for a refund of the difference between the charged fare and the optimal route fare.

### 3.2 Professional Conduct
Drivers must:
- Maintain professional and courteous behavior at all times
- Not discriminate against riders based on race, gender, religion, or any other protected characteristic
- Arrive at the pickup location within the estimated time
- Not refuse a booking without valid reason after acceptance
- Keep the vehicle clean and in roadworthy condition

### 3.3 No-Show Policy (Driver)
If a driver accepts a booking but fails to arrive at the pickup location within **10 minutes** of the scheduled time without communicating with the rider, the driver may be marked as a "no-show" and:
- The rider will not be charged any cancellation fee
- The driver's acceptance rate score may be affected
- Repeated no-shows may result in account suspension

### 3.4 Zero Commission Model
Ryde operates on a **0% commission** model for drivers. Drivers receive the full fare amount, minus applicable platform service fees (if any) and government-mandated taxes. Drivers are classified as independent contractors, not employees.

## 4. Cancellation Policy

### 4.1 Rider Cancellation
- **Free cancellation**: Rider may cancel for free within **2 minutes** of booking, before a driver is assigned.
- **After driver assignment**: A cancellation fee of **$5.00 SGD** applies if the rider cancels after a driver has been assigned, unless the driver is significantly delayed (more than 10 minutes beyond estimated arrival).
- **No-show by rider**: If the rider does not appear at the pickup location within the grace period, a no-show fee of **$8.00 SGD** applies.

### 4.2 Driver Cancellation
- Drivers may cancel without penalty if the rider is not at the pickup location after the grace period.
- Drivers who cancel without valid reason after acceptance may see their acceptance rate and rating affected.
- Repeated unjustified cancellations may result in account review.

### 4.3 Refund Processing
- Approved refunds are processed within **3-5 business days** to the original payment method.
- Refunds for route deviation disputes are calculated as the difference between the actual charged fare and the estimated optimal route fare.

## 5. Fare Structure and Surge Pricing

### 5.1 Base Fare Components
The fare consists of:
- **Base fare**: Fixed starting amount
- **Distance fare**: Per-kilometer charge
- **Time fare**: Per-minute charge (applies when vehicle is moving below a certain speed or stationary)
- **Surge multiplier**: Applied during high-demand periods (displayed to rider before booking)

### 5.2 Surge Pricing Transparency
- Surge pricing is clearly displayed before the rider confirms the booking
- The rider must accept the surge price before the booking is confirmed
- Disputes regarding surge pricing will only be upheld if the surge was not displayed at the time of booking

### 5.3 Promo Codes and Discounts
- Promo codes must be applied before the ride is confirmed
- Promo codes cannot be applied retroactively after the ride is completed
- Ryde+ subscribers receive priority matching and cashback rewards as per their subscription terms

## 6. Ratings and Reviews

### 6.1 Two-Way Rating System
Both riders and drivers rate each other after each trip on a scale of 1-5 stars.

### 6.2 Rating Retaliation
Rating retaliation (giving an unfairly low rating in response to receiving a low rating) is prohibited. Patterns of retaliatory ratings may result in account review.

### 6.3 Low Rating Impact
- Drivers with an average rating below **4.3 stars** over 50+ trips may be subject to account review
- Riders with consistently low ratings may have their booking priority reduced

## 7. Dispute Resolution

### 7.1 Filing a Dispute
Riders or drivers may file a dispute through the Ryde app within **7 days** of the trip. The dispute must include:
- A description of the issue
- The order/booking ID
- Any supporting evidence (photos, screenshots, etc.)

### 7.2 Dispute Categories
1. **Route Deviation** — Driver took a longer route resulting in overcharging
2. **No-Show Charge** — Rider or driver did not appear; cancellation fee disputed
3. **Property Damage / Mess** — Rider caused damage or mess to vehicle; cleaning fee claimed
4. **Safety Incident** — Driver or rider behaved inappropriately or unsafely during the trip
5. **Fare Dispute** — Overcharging, surge pricing, promo code issues
6. **Service Quality** — Rudeness, detours, rating retaliation

### 7.3 Dispute Resolution Timeline
- Standard disputes: Resolved within **24-72 hours**
- Safety incidents: Escalated immediately to human review (P0 priority)
- Refund processing: **3-5 business days** after dispute resolution

### 7.4 Evidence in Disputes
The following evidence may be used in dispute resolution:
- GPS telemetry data (actual route vs. optimal route)
- In-app chat logs between rider and driver
- Payment and fare breakdown records
- Trip timestamps (scheduled, actual start, actual end)
- Rider and driver historical behavior profiles
- Photos (for property damage disputes)

## 8. Safety Standards

### 8.1 Driver Verification
All drivers must pass:

- Vehicle inspection and roadworthiness certification

### 8.2 Safety Incident Reporting
Safety incidents (inappropriate behavior, harassment, reckless driving) must be reported immediately. These are classified as **P0 priority** and require human review.

### 8.3 Insurance
Ryde maintains commercial vehicle insurance coverage for trips booked through the platform. Drivers must maintain valid personal vehicle insurance.
