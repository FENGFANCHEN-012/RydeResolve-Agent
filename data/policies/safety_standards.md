# Ryde Safety Standards

## 1. Overview

Safety is Ryde's highest priority. This document defines the safety standards, incident classification, and response protocols for all users of the Ryde platform.

## 2. Safety Incident Classification

### 2.1 P0 — Critical Safety Incidents (Immediate Human Review)
- Physical assault or threat of violence
- Sexual harassment or misconduct
- Reckless driving endangering life
- Driving under the influence (alcohol or drugs)
- Stalking or following after trip completion
- Hate speech or discrimination

**Response**: All P0 incidents are immediately escalated to human review. The involved driver's account may be temporarily suspended pending investigation. Criminal matters are reported to Singapore Police.

### 2.2 P1 — High Priority Safety Concerns
- Verbal abuse or aggressive behavior
- Unsafe driving (speeding, sudden stops, erratic lane changes)
- Refusal to follow safety instructions (e.g., rider refusing seatbelt)
- Driver appearing fatigued or unwell

**Response**: Reviewed within 24 hours. Driver may receive a formal warning. Pattern of P1 incidents may escalate to P0.

### 2.3 P2 — Medium Priority
- Inappropriate conversation topics
- Refusal to follow requested route (without safety justification)
- Unprofessional appearance or hygiene
- Minor vehicle safety issues (non-functioning seatbelt, etc.)

### 2.4 P3 — Low Priority
- Minor service quality issues
- Cleanliness complaints
- Punctuality concerns

## 3. GPS Telemetry Safety Monitoring

### 3.1 Monitored Metrics
Ryde's platform monitors the following GPS-derived safety metrics:
- **Speed**: Vehicle speed vs. posted speed limit
- **Acceleration/Deceleration**: Sudden acceleration or hard braking events
- **Route adherence**: Deviation from optimal route without notification
- **Stop patterns**: Unexpected or prolonged stops
- **Trip duration**: Actual duration vs. estimated duration

### 3.2 Automated Flags
The system automatically flags:
- Speed exceeding limit by 20+ km/h
- 3+ hard braking events in a single trip
- Route deviation exceeding 10% without chat notification
- Stops longer than 5 minutes (excluding traffic)

## 4. Safety Incident Reporting

### 4.1 How to Report
Both riders and drivers can report safety incidents:
- Through the Ryde app (Safety > Report Incident)
- Via the emergency button in the app (for immediate threats)
- By emailing safety@rydesharing.com

### 4.2 Required Information
- Incident description (what happened, when, where)
- Booking/order ID
- Photos or screenshots (if applicable)
- Witness contact (if available)

### 4.3 Emergency Protocol
For immediate danger:
1. Call Singapore Police at 999 (or 995 for medical emergency)
2. Use the Ryde in-app emergency button to alert Ryde's safety team
3. Do not confront the other party
4. If safe to do so, exit the vehicle and move to a safe location

## 5. Driver Safety Screening

### 5.1 Pre-Registration Screening
- Criminal record check (via Singapore Police Force)
- Driving license verification (LTA)
- Vehicle inspection certification
- Medical fitness declaration

### 5.2 Ongoing Monitoring
- Periodic re-screening (every 12 months)
- Continuous monitoring of rider safety reports
- GPS telemetry pattern analysis
- Rating threshold monitoring (below 4.3 stars triggers review)

## 6. Insurance Coverage

### 6.1 Commercial Insurance
Ryde maintains commercial vehicle insurance covering:
- Third-party bodily injury
- Third-party property damage
- Passenger injury during a booked trip

### 6.2 Driver Insurance Requirements
Drivers must maintain:
- Valid personal vehicle insurance (minimum Third-Party coverage)
- Road tax validity
- LTA inspection certification (if vehicle is 3+ years old)

### 6.3 Accident Claims
In case of an accident during a booked trip:
1. All parties should seek medical attention if injured
2. Take photos of the scene and any damage
3. File a police report if required by law
4. Report the incident through the Ryde app within 24 hours
5. Ryde's insurance team will coordinate the claim process
