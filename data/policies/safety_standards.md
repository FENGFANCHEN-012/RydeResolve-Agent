## 1. Overview

Safety is Ryde's highest priority. This document defines the safety standards, incident classification, screening procedures, and response protocols for all users of the Ryde platform. Ryde enforces high driver community standards and comprehensive review systems to ensure rider safety.

## 2. Safety Incident Classification

### 2.1 P0 — Critical Safety Incidents (Immediate Human Review)

- Physical assault or threat of violence
- Sexual harassment or misconduct
- Reckless driving endangering life
- Driving under the influence (alcohol or drugs)
- Stalking or following after trip completion
- Hate speech or discrimination

**Response:** All P0 incidents are immediately escalated to human review. The involved driver's account may be temporarily suspended pending investigation. Criminal matters are reported to Singapore Police.

### 2.2 P1 — High Priority Safety Concerns

- Verbal abuse or aggressive behavior
- Unsafe driving (speeding, sudden stops, erratic lane changes)
- Refusal to follow safety instructions (e.g., rider refusing seatbelt)
- Driver appearing fatigued or unwell

**Response:** Reviewed within 24 hours. Driver may receive a formal warning. Pattern of P1 incidents may escalate to P0.

### 2.3 P2 — Medium Priority

- Inappropriate conversation topics
- Refusal to follow requested route (without safety justification)
- Unprofessional appearance or hygiene
- Minor vehicle safety issues (non-functioning seatbelt, etc.)

### 2.4 P3 — Low Priority

- Minor service quality issues
- Cleanliness complaints
- Punctuality concerns

## 3. Code of Conduct: Safety First Rule

### 3.1 Driver Standards

- Must adhere to all driving rules and regulations at all times.
- Must keep to speed limits.
- Must obey road signs.
- Must refrain from using their phones while on the road.

### 3.2 Rider Expectations

- Must have their seatbelts on.
- When traveling with a child below the height of 1.35m, parents/guardians are required to ensure the child is secured with a child restraint appropriate for their height and weight.
- Riders are strongly encouraged to practice general safety and prudence.

## 4. Safe Driving Best Practices

### 4.1 Pre-Trip Verification

- Always confirm and check that the passenger's name and destination match the information displayed in the Ryde Driver App before starting the trip.
- Follow the rules of designated pick-up or drop-off (PUDO) points and ensure the location is legally allowed for private ride-hailing.

### 4.2 Focused Driving

- Never hold or text on your phone while driving.
- Utilize a dashboard mount or holder to answer calls, ensuring the device does not obstruct your view of the road.

### 4.3 Vehicle Maintenance

- Follow the servicing advice from your vehicle manufacturer or retailer to spot issues early.
- Perform required regular inspections to stay roadworthy, checking schedules via the LTA website.
- If you notice unusual sounds or wearing parts, get them checked and fixed early.
- Always replace vehicle parts with those approved by the manufacturer.
- Hold on to all receipts and service notes for future reference.
- Routinely inspect tire pressure, fluid levels, brakes, proper lighting, and functioning windshield wipers.

### 4.4 Weather Safety

Given Singapore's unpredictable weather:

- Download and use the myENV app (by NEA).
- Monitor WBGT readings (Wet Bulb Globe Temperature) to avoid heat stress.
- Check lightning alerts to take precautions during thunderstorms.
- Check the app regularly, especially when waiting outdoors or driving in exposed areas.

### 4.5 Driver Fatigue Management

- Take regular breaks to stay alert and avoid fatigue.
- If you feel tired or drowsy, pause and rest before continuing to drive.
- Ensure you get adequate rest to stay safe and serve passengers effectively.

## 5. Platform Work Safety and Fairness

### 5.1 Rules Drivers Must Follow

- Only accept rides or delivery jobs officially assigned through the registered Ryde platform.
- Driver accounts are strictly personal — no sharing or renting.
- Keep documents updated: driving licence, vehicle details, and insurance must always be current and valid.
- Obey traffic laws, drive safely, and prioritise own safety as well as the safety of passengers and the public.
- Report any suspicious or illegal platform work.

### 5.2 Prohibited Behaviors

- No off-platform jobs — must not accept private rides that bypass the approved platform system.
- No account sharing or renting — classified as illegal and subject to enforcement action.
- No unauthorized operation — must not operate without valid registrations or permits.
- No promotion of illegal services — must not encourage or participate in any unlicensed or unauthorized ride-hailing.

### 5.3 Penalties for Violations

- Monetary fines for providing or assisting in illegal services.
- Temporary suspension or permanent ban from platform work.
- Vehicle impoundment in serious cases.
- Action against the driver's operating licence by authorities.

### 5.4 External Safety References

- WSH (Workplace Safety and Health) Council: "Guide to Safe Driving," video resources on managing fatigue and essential safety tips for platform workers.
- Land Transport Authority (LTA): Refer to the LTA website for active mobility rules and codes of conduct.

## 6. In-Vehicle Recording

### 6.1 Ryde-Installed Recording

- Ryde may install in-vehicle audio/video recording devices for safety and security purposes.
- These recordings may be used for safety monitoring, detecting unsafe driving behavior, fraud prevention, and crime prosecution.

### 6.2 Partner-Installed Recording

- Personal in-vehicle cameras installed by partners are the partner's responsibility.
- Partners must comply with applicable laws regarding recording, including obtaining consent where required.

## 7. Driver Safety Screening

### 7.1 Pre-Registration Screening

- Criminal record check (via Singapore Police Force).
- Driving license verification (LTA).
- Vehicle inspection certification.
- Medical fitness declaration.

### 7.2 Ongoing Monitoring

- Periodic re-screening (every 12 months).
- Continuous monitoring of rider safety reports.
- Rating threshold monitoring (below 4.3 stars triggers review).

## 8. Insurance Coverage

### 8.1 Mandatory Insurance

- Partners must possess all mandatory insurance policies related to their provision of the Services in the jurisdiction in which they operate.
- Partners must have a valid policy/liability insurance (in industry-standard coverage amounts).
- Partners are solely responsible for any and all claims, judgments, and liabilities resulting from any accident, loss, or damage, including personal injuries, death, and property damage.

### 8.2 Vehicle Insurance

- Drivers must maintain valid personal vehicle insurance (minimum Third-Party coverage).
- Road tax must be valid.
- LTA inspection certification must be current (if vehicle is 3+ years old).

### 8.3 Accident Claims

In case of an accident during a booked trip:

1. All parties should seek medical attention if injured.
2. Take photos of the scene and any damage.
3. File a police report if required by law.
4. Report the incident through the Ryde app within 24 hours.
5. Ryde's insurance team will coordinate the claim process.

## 9. Safety Incident Reporting

### 9.1 How to Report

Both riders and drivers can report safety incidents:

- Through the Ryde app (Safety > Report Incident).
- By emailing safety@rydesharing.com.
- By emailing support@rydesharing.com.

### 9.2 Required Information

- Incident description (what happened, when, where).
- Booking/order ID.
- Photos or screenshots (if applicable).
- Witness contact (if available).

### 9.3 Emergency Protocol

For immediate danger:

1. Call Singapore Police at 999 (or 995 for medical emergency).
2. Use the Ryde in-app emergency button to alert Ryde's safety team.
3. Do not confront the other party.
4. If safe to do so, exit the vehicle and move to a safe location.

### 9.4 Reporting Channels for Illegal Activity

- Illegal Ride-Hailing Services: go.gov.sg/report-illegal-rides
- Illegal Platform Delivery Work: go.gov.sg/report-illegal-platform-delivery-work
- General Infringement Reporting: Ministry of Manpower (MOM) — mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/report-an-infringement
- Direct to Ryde: Through the Ryde Driver App or support@rydesharing.com

All reports are kept confidential.

## 10. Mutual Respect and Prohibited Conduct

### 10.1 Zero Tolerance

- Physical and verbal assault or harassment is strictly not tolerated on the platform.
- Drivers must avoid passing comments on anyone's appearance.
- Drivers must not make remarks on race, religion, or other forms of personal beliefs.
- Contacting the passenger after the trip has ended is strictly prohibited.

### 10.2 Rider Conduct

- Passengers must avoid slurs or personal insults.
- Passengers should be respectful and constructive in feedback.
- Passengers must not cause or attempt to cause damage to the Partner or the Partner's property.
