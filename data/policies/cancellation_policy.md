# Ryde Cancellation Policy

## 1. Overview

This policy governs cancellations by both riders and drivers on the Ryde platform. It defines the conditions under which cancellation fees apply, grace periods, and the dispute process for cancellation-related charges.

## 2. Rider-Initiated Cancellations

### 2.1 Free Cancellation Window
- Riders may cancel a booking **free of charge** within **2 minutes** of placing the booking, provided a driver has not yet been assigned.
- Once a driver has been assigned and is en route to the pickup, the free cancellation window closes.

### 2.2 Cancellation Fee After Driver Assignment
If a rider cancels after a driver has been assigned:
- A cancellation fee of **$5.00 SGD** applies.
- **Exception**: If the driver is more than **10 minutes delayed** beyond the estimated arrival time, the rider may cancel without penalty.
- **Exception**: If the driver cancels first, no rider cancellation fee applies.

### 2.3 No-Show by Rider
If the rider does not appear at the designated pickup location:
- **Standard grace period**: 5 minutes from driver arrival
- **Ryde+ grace period**: 10 minutes from driver arrival
- After the grace period, the driver may mark the rider as a "no-show"
- A no-show fee of **$8.00 SGD** is charged to the rider
- The driver receives a partial compensation for time and fuel spent

### 2.4 Disputing a No-Show Charge
A rider may dispute a no-show charge if:
- The rider was present at the pickup location but the driver did not arrive
- The driver arrived at the wrong location
- The driver marked "no-show" prematurely (before the grace period expired)
- The rider attempted to contact the driver but received no response

**Evidence required**: Chat logs, GPS location of rider at pickup time, attempted call records.

## 3. Driver-Initiated Cancellations

### 3.1 Valid Reasons for Cancellation
Drivers may cancel a booking without penalty if:
- The rider is not at the pickup location after the grace period
- The rider behaves inappropriately or is intoxicated to the point of safety concern
- The rider requests an illegal or unsafe destination
- The driver experiences a vehicle breakdown or medical emergency (documentation required)

### 3.2 Invalid Cancellations
Cancellations without valid reason after acceptance may result in:
- Impact on the driver's acceptance rate metric
- Temporary reduction in booking priority
- Account review for repeated unjustified cancellations (3+ in a 7-day period)

### 3.3 Driver No-Show
If a driver accepts a booking but:
- Does not arrive within **10 minutes** of the scheduled pickup time, AND
- Does not communicate with the rider about the delay

Then:
- The rider is **not charged** any cancellation fee
- The rider may rebook at no additional cost
- The driver's no-show is recorded in their behavior profile
- Repeated driver no-shows (3+ in 30 days) may result in account suspension

## 4. Disputing Cancellation Fees

### 4.1 Filing a Cancellation Dispute
A rider may file a cancellation dispute within **7 days** of the charge. The dispute should include:
- Booking/order ID
- Reason for disputing the charge
- Any supporting evidence (chat logs, location data, timestamps)

### 4.2 Valid Dispute Grounds for No-Show Charges
| Ground | Description | Required Evidence |
|--------|-------------|-------------------|
| Rider present at pickup | Rider was at the location within grace period | GPS location, timestamp |
| Wrong pickup location | Driver went to wrong address | GPS trace of driver vs. pickup point |
| Premature no-show marking | Driver marked no-show before grace period expired | Timestamp comparison |
| No contact from driver | Rider waited and driver did not communicate | Call log, chat log |

### 4.3 Resolution Outcomes
| Outcome | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Full refund | Cancellation fee fully refunded to rider |
| Partial refund | Portion of fee refunded (e.g., if driver was slightly delayed) |
| Charge upheld | Cancellation fee is valid; no refund issued |
| Driver penalty | Driver's behavior flagged; repeated incidents may lead to account review |

## 5. Refund Processing

- Approved refunds are processed within **3-5 business days**
- Refunds are returned to the original payment method
- Ryde+ subscribers receive expedited refund processing (1-2 business days)
