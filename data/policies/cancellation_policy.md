# Ryde Cancellation and Waiting Time Policy

**Source:** Ryde Help Center — Cancellation and Waiting Time Policy
**URL:** https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/20244064619161-Cancellation-and-Waiting-Time-Policy
**Publisher:** Ryde Technologies Pte. Ltd.

## 1. Overview

This policy governs cancellations by both riders and drivers on the Ryde platform. It defines the conditions under which cancellation fees apply, waiting time fees, grace periods, and the dispute process for cancellation-related charges.

## 2. Cancellation Fee Structure

### 2.1 Rider-Initiated Cancellations

- Riders may cancel a request for transportation/delivery at any time before the ride commences.
- If a user cancels a booking or does not show up, they may be charged a Cancellation Fee.
- The cancellation fee amount depends on the service type and the stage of the trip.

### 2.2 When Cancellation Fees Apply

A cancellation fee may be charged to the rider when:

- The rider cancels after a driver has been assigned and is en route.
- The rider does not show up at the pickup location within the grace period.
- The rider booked the wrong service or wrong location and refused to cancel.
- The recipient is unreachable or uncontactable (for RydeSEND).

### 2.3 When Cancellation Fees Do Not Apply

A cancellation fee should not be charged when:

- The rider cancels within the free cancellation window (before driver assignment).
- The driver cancels first.
- The driver is significantly delayed (more than 10 minutes beyond estimated arrival time).
- The driver arrives at the wrong location.
- The driver marks "no-show" prematurely (before the grace period expires).

## 3. Waiting Time Policy

### 3.1 Grace Periods

- **Standard grace period**: 3 minutes from driver arrival at pickup location.
- The driver must have arrived at the pickup location and the customer must not be present within 3 minutes of arrival for a cancellation/waiting fee to apply.

### 3.2 Waiting Time Fee

- Drivers may key in a Waiting Time Fee if the rider is not present at the pickup point within the grace period.
- The waiting time fee compensates the driver for time and fuel spent waiting.

### 3.3 RydeSEND Specific

- If the recipient is unreachable or uncontactable after 4 minutes from the Partner's arrival, the user remains liable to pay the delivery booking fee in full.
- If a RydeSEND order was delayed for more than 3 minutes and no waiting fee was received, the driver can file an appeal.
- If a RydeSEND order was cancelled after 3 minutes and no cancellation fee was received, the driver can file an appeal.

## 4. Driver Cancellation Rules

### 4.1 Acceptable Cancellation Reasons

Cancellations must be based on acceptable reasons shown in the Application. Acceptable reasons include:

- Rider is not at the pickup location after the grace period.
- Rider behaves inappropriately or is intoxicated to the point of safety concern.
- Rider requests an illegal or unsafe destination.
- Vehicle breakdown or medical emergency (documentation required).
- Rider booked the wrong service or wrong location and refused to cancel.
- Rider came with additional riders beyond what was indicated.
- Rider arrived with a child without a booster seat.

### 4.2 Unacceptable Cancellations

- High and/or frequent cancellation rates impair the User experience and negatively impact Ryde.
- Unacceptable cancellations or ignoring bookings may result in temporary suspension of access to the Service.
- Cancellations without valid reason after acceptance may impact the driver's acceptance rate and rating.

### 4.3 Driver Cancellation Appeal

Drivers can appeal for cancellation or waiting fees under the following circumstances:

1. Rider booked the wrong service or wrong location and refused to cancel.
2. Rider cancelled the trip once the driver reached the pickup point.
3. Rider came with additional riders.
4. Rider arrived with a child without a booster seat.
5. RydeSEND order was delayed for more than 3 minutes and no waiting fee was received.
6. RydeSEND order was cancelled after 3 minutes and no cancellation fee was received.
7. Driver cancelled a RydeSEND trip because the order wasn't ready after 3 minutes but no cancellation fee was received.

**Eligibility Criteria:** To qualify for a cancellation/waiting fee, the driver must have arrived at the pickup location and the customer was not present within 3 minutes of arrival. If a customer cancels, the fee is automatically credited if the driver meets eligibility.

## 5. Disputing a Cancellation Fee

### 5.1 Filing a Cancellation Dispute

A rider may file a cancellation dispute if they feel they were incorrectly charged. They may contact support@rydesharing.com. The dispute should include:

- Booking/order ID
- Reason for disputing the charge
- Any supporting evidence (chat logs, location data, timestamps)

### 5.2 Valid Dispute Grounds for No-Show Charges

| Ground | Description | Required Evidence |
|--------|-------------|-------------------|
| Rider present at pickup | Rider was at the location within grace period | GPS location, timestamp |
| Wrong pickup location | Driver went to wrong address | GPS trace of driver vs. pickup point |
| Premature no-show marking | Driver marked no-show before grace period expired | Timestamp comparison |
| No contact from driver | Rider waited and driver did not communicate | Call log, chat log |
| Driver significantly delayed | Driver was more than 10 minutes beyond estimated arrival | Timestamp, GPS trace |

### 5.3 Resolution Outcomes

| Outcome | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Full refund | Cancellation fee fully refunded to rider |
| Partial refund | Portion of fee refunded (e.g., if driver was slightly delayed) |
| Charge upheld | Cancellation fee is valid; no refund issued |
| Driver penalty | Driver's behavior flagged; repeated incidents may lead to account review |

## 6. ERP (Electronic Road Pricing) Charge Claims

Drivers who forgot to include ERP charges in fares can file a claim with:

- Name of ERP Gantry
- Time of Entry
- Screenshot of Cashcard Statement

**Important:** RydePOOL, RydeHIRE, and RydeSEND trips already include ERP — no separate claim is needed for these services.

## 7. Refund Processing

- Refunds are at Ryde's absolute discretion.
- Refunds may be credited to the payment card, RydeCoins wallet, or another reasonable method.
- Approved refunds are processed within 3-5 business days.
- RydeCoins and Vouchers are strictly non-refundable, non-transferable, and cannot be exchanged for cash.

## 8. Account Consequences

### 8.1 For Drivers

- Unacceptable cancellations or ignoring bookings may result in temporary suspension of access to the Service.
- Repeated unjustified cancellations (3+ in a 7-day period) may trigger account review.
- Drivers with high cancellation rates may see reduced booking priority.

### 8.2 For Riders

- Non-payment of verified cancellation fees may result in temporary suspension or permanent deactivation.
- Repeated unjustified cancellations may result in reduced booking priority.
- Habitual booking and cancelling is monitored as "booking abuse."
