# Ryde Dispute Resolution Guide

**Purpose:** This guide synthesizes Ryde's official policies to provide clear guidance for the 7 core dispute scenarios. It maps each scenario to the relevant official policy clauses and explains how disputes should be resolved based on Ryde's Terms of Use, Help Center articles, and Code of Conduct.

**Sources:**
- Ryde Terms of Use: https://rydesharing.com/terms-of-use/
- Ryde Help Center: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us
- Ryde Code of Conduct: https://rydesharing.com/code-of-conduct/
- Ryde Privacy Policy: https://rydesharing.com/privacy-policy/

## Scenario 1: Route Deviation (Driver Takes Longer Route)

### What the Rider Claims
The driver took a longer route than necessary, resulting in a higher fare than expected.

### Relevant Official Policies

**From Terms of Use — Driver Obligations:**
- Drivers must obey all local laws related to their provision of the Services.
- Drivers are solely responsible for any violations of local laws.
- Drivers shall not contact the User for purposes other than in connection with the Service.

**From Terms of Use — Rider Obligations:**
- Riders must indicate the accurate number of passengers when requesting a Service.
- Riders shall not contact the Partner for purposes other than the Service.

**From Driver Guidelines — Safe Driving:**
- Drivers must adhere to all driving rules and regulations at all times.
- Drivers must keep to speed limits and obey road signs.
- Drivers must travel directly to the rider's pick-up location once they have accepted the trip.

**From Code of Conduct — Communicate Rule:**
- If passengers have additional requests (e.g., a preferred travel route), they must communicate this with the driver and come to a mutual agreement prior to starting the trip.

### How to Resolve

1. **Check GPS telemetry data** — Compare the actual route taken vs. the optimal route.
2. **Check in-app chat logs** — Did the rider request a specific route? Did the driver inform the rider of a detour due to traffic/road closures?
3. **Determine fault:**
   - **Driver at fault**: Took a clearly longer route without justification or communication. Rider may be eligible for a fare adjustment/refund at Ryde's discretion.
   - **Rider at fault**: Requested a specific route or failed to communicate preferences. No refund.
   - **Shared fault**: Traffic/road conditions justified a detour but driver failed to communicate. Partial adjustment may be appropriate.
4. **Refund authority**: Ryde retains absolute discretion over refunds. Contact support@rydesharing.com.

### Key Evidence
- GPS route trace (actual vs. optimal).
- In-app chat logs between rider and driver.
- Trip timestamps.
- Rider's stated preferences at booking.

## Scenario 2: No-Show Charge (Rider Charged But Was Present)

### What the Rider Claims
The driver marked the rider as a no-show and charged a cancellation fee, but the rider was actually at the pickup location.

### Relevant Official Policies

**From Cancellation Policy:**
- Grace period: 3 minutes from driver arrival at pickup location.
- The driver must have arrived at the pickup location and the customer must not be present within 3 minutes of arrival for a cancellation/waiting fee to apply.
- If a customer cancels, the fee is automatically credited if the driver meets eligibility.

**From Cancellation Policy — Valid Dispute Grounds:**
| Ground | Description | Required Evidence |
|--------|-------------|-------------------|
| Rider present at pickup | Rider was at the location within grace period | GPS location, timestamp |
| Wrong pickup location | Driver went to wrong address | GPS trace of driver vs. pickup point |
| Premature no-show marking | Driver marked no-show before grace period expired | Timestamp comparison |
| No contact from driver | Rider waited and driver did not communicate | Call log, chat log |
| Driver significantly delayed | Driver was more than 10 minutes beyond estimated arrival | Timestamp, GPS trace |

**From Terms of Use — Refunds:**
- Ryde retains absolute discretion over refunds for cancellation fees.
- Refunds may be credited to the payment card, RydeCoins wallet, or another reasonable method.

### How to Resolve

1. **Verify driver arrival time** — Did the driver actually arrive at the correct pickup location?
2. **Verify rider presence** — Was the rider at the pickup location within the 3-minute grace period?
3. **Check timestamps** — Was the no-show marked before or after the grace period expired?
4. **Determine fault:**
   - **Driver at fault**: Marked no-show prematurely, went to wrong location, or failed to communicate. Full refund of cancellation fee.
   - **Rider at fault**: Not present within grace period, failed to communicate delay. Charge upheld.
   - **Shared fault**: Driver slightly delayed, rider slightly late. Partial refund may be appropriate.
5. **Driver consequences**: Repeated premature no-show markings may lead to account review.

### Key Evidence
- GPS location of rider at pickup time.
- GPS trace of driver vs. pickup point.
- Timestamp comparison (driver arrival vs. no-show marking vs. grace period).
- Call log / chat log.

## Scenario 3: Fare Dispute (Price Calculation Error / Surge Pricing)

### What the Rider Claims
The fare charged was incorrect — either a calculation error or surge pricing was not properly displayed.

### Relevant Official Policies

**From Terms of Use — Payments:**
- Users must make full payment of User Charges at the time of booking via cash or automated payment methods (credit/debit card, Apple Pay, RydeCoins).
- Payments are non-refundable and irrevocable.
- Ryde may place a reasonable authorization hold to verify the payment method.
- Ryde charges a Service Fee of up to 20% of the User Charges (for Partners).

**From Terms of Use — Refunds:**
- Ryde retains absolute discretion over refunds.
- RydeCoins and Vouchers are strictly non-refundable, non-transferable, and cannot be exchanged for cash.

**From Terms of Use — Limitation of Liability:**
- Any claims against Ryde shall be limited to the aggregate amount of all amounts actually paid by/due from the user in utilizing the Service during the event giving rise to the claim.

### How to Resolve

1. **Verify the fare breakdown** — Base fare, distance, time, tolls, surge multiplier, service fee.
2. **Check surge pricing display** — Was the surge multiplier clearly displayed before booking confirmation?
3. **Check for calculation errors** — Are the distance/time charges consistent with the trip data?
4. **Determine fault:**
   - **System error**: Fare calculation was objectively wrong (e.g., wrong distance used). Full refund of overcharge.
   - **Surge not displayed**: Rider has evidence (screenshot) that surge was not shown. Refund of surge amount.
   - **Rider accepted surge**: Surge was clearly displayed and rider confirmed. Charge upheld.
   - **Disputed distance**: GPS shows different distance than charged. Adjust to actual distance.
5. **Refund authority**: Ryde retains absolute discretion. Contact support@rydesharing.com.

### Key Evidence
- Fare breakdown from the app.
- Screenshot of booking confirmation (showing surge display).
- GPS telemetry (actual distance travelled).
- Payment receipt.

## Scenario 4: Cancellation Refund (Rider Wants Cancellation Fee Back)

### What the Rider Claims
The rider was charged a cancellation fee but believes they should not have been charged.

### Relevant Official Policies

**From Cancellation Policy:**
- Riders may cancel a request for transportation/delivery at any time before the ride commences.
- If a user cancels a booking or does not show up, they may be charged a Cancellation Fee.
- If a user feels they were incorrectly charged, they may contact support@rydesharing.com.
- Refunds are at Ryde's absolute discretion.

**From Cancellation Policy — Eligibility for Cancellation/Waiting Fee:**
- The driver must have arrived at the pickup location.
- The customer was not present within 3 minutes of arrival.
- If a customer cancels, the fee is automatically credited if the driver meets eligibility.

**From Terms of Use — Refunds:**
- Refunds may be credited to the payment card, RydeCoins wallet, or another reasonable method.

### How to Resolve

1. **Determine cancellation timing** — When did the rider cancel relative to driver assignment?
2. **Check driver status** — Had the driver been assigned? Was the driver en route?
3. **Check grace period** — If no-show charge, was the rider present within 3 minutes?
4. **Determine fault:**
   - **Rider cancelled before driver assignment**: No fee should apply. Full refund.
   - **Rider cancelled after driver assignment, driver not delayed**: Cancellation fee valid. Charge upheld.
   - **Rider cancelled after driver assignment, driver delayed >10 min**: No fee. Full refund.
   - **No-show charge, rider was present**: Full refund (see Scenario 2).
5. **Refund processing**: 3-5 business days to original payment method.

### Key Evidence
- Booking timestamp vs. cancellation timestamp.
- Driver assignment timestamp.
- Driver arrival timestamp vs. estimated arrival.
- GPS location of rider at pickup time.

## Scenario 5: Service Quality (Driver Rudeness / Unsafe Driving)

### What the Rider Claims
The driver was rude, drove unsafely, or provided poor service quality.

### Relevant Official Policies

**From Code of Conduct — Rule 4: Mutual Respect:**
- Drivers must be kind to their passenger.
- Physical and verbal assault or harassment is strictly not tolerated.
- Drivers must avoid passing comments on anyone's appearance.
- Drivers must not make remarks on race, religion, or other forms of personal beliefs.

**From Safety Standards — P0 Critical Safety Incidents:**
- Physical assault or threat of violence.
- Sexual harassment or misconduct.
- Reckless driving endangering life.
- Driving under the influence.
- **Response**: Immediately escalated to human review. Account may be temporarily suspended. Criminal matters reported to Singapore Police.

**From Safety Standards — P1 High Priority:**
- Verbal abuse or aggressive behavior.
- Unsafe driving (speeding, sudden stops, erratic lane changes).
- **Response**: Reviewed within 24 hours. Formal warning. Pattern may escalate to P0.

**From Driver Performance Standards:**
- Drivers are rated by riders on a scale of 1-5 stars.
- Account may be placed on hold if rating falls below service standard.
- Upon reinstatement, ratings reset to 4.90.

**From Ratings Moderation Policy:**
- Reviews may be removed if they violate Terms of Use, are biased/fraudulent, or irrelevant.
- Commentary about social/political/religious views, profanity, or circumstances outside driver's control should be avoided.

### How to Resolve

1. **Classify severity**:
   - **P0 (Critical)**: Physical assault, sexual harassment, reckless driving, DUI. Immediate human escalation.
   - **P1 (High)**: Verbal abuse, unsafe driving. Review within 24 hours.
   - **P2/P3 (Medium/Low)**: Inappropriate conversation, unprofessional appearance, minor issues.
2. **Check evidence**:
   - Rider rating and review.
   - GPS telemetry (speeding, hard braking, erratic driving).
   - In-app chat logs.
   - Photos/videos if applicable.
3. **Determine action**:
   - **P0**: Immediate account suspension, report to police if criminal.
   - **P1**: Formal warning. Pattern of P1 incidents may escalate to P0.
   - **P2/P3**: Feedback to driver. No immediate action unless pattern.
4. **Rider compensation**: At Ryde's discretion — may include partial fare refund, Ryde+ credit, or formal apology.

### Key Evidence
- Rider rating and review.
- GPS telemetry (speed, acceleration, route).
- In-app chat logs.
- Witness statements (if any).
- Photos/videos.

## Scenario 6: Property Damage (Rider Damaged Vehicle)

### What the Driver Claims
The rider caused damage or mess to the vehicle (vomit, stains, spillage, physical damage).

### Relevant Official Policies

**From Terms of Use — Repair and Cleaning Fees:**
- Users are responsible for the cost of repairing damage or necessary cleaning of the Partner's property resulting from misuse or breach of Terms.
- Riders may be subject to a cleaning fee of up to S$200 for incidents requiring professional cleaning (e.g., stains, bodily fluids, vomit).
- Non-payment of verified cleaning fees may result in temporary suspension or permanent deactivation.

**From Driver Guidelines — Cleaning Claims:**
Drivers can appeal for cleaning compensation for:
1. Vomit
2. Seat stains
3. Spillage
4. Soil

**Required evidence:**
- Photo evidence (picture of damage with timestamp).
- Receipt from Cleaning Company including:
  - Name, contact number & address.
  - Date of cleaning.
  - Authorised personnel's signature/company stamp.

**From Refund Policy — Cleaning Fee Disputes:**
A rider may dispute a cleaning/repair fee if:
- The damage was pre-existing.
- The photo timestamp does not match the trip time.
- The claimed amount is disproportionate to the damage.
- The damage was caused by a previous rider.

### How to Resolve

1. **Verify the claim**:
   - Did the driver provide photo evidence with timestamp?
   - Did the driver provide a cleaning/repair receipt?
   - Does the photo timestamp match the trip time?
2. **Check for pre-existing damage**:
   - Was the vehicle already damaged before the trip?
   - Did the rider note any pre-existing damage at the start?
3. **Assess proportionality**:
   - Is the claimed amount reasonable for the damage?
   - Minor spill: S$20-50. Vomit: S$50-150. Professional detailing: S$50-100. Physical damage: actual cost.
4. **Determine outcome**:
   - **Claim valid**: Charge rider up to S$200 (or actual cost for physical damage). Deduct from payment method.
   - **Claim invalid**: Pre-existing damage, wrong timestamp, or disproportionate amount. Fee waived.
   - **Shared responsibility**: Partial charge based on evidence.
5. **Non-payment consequences**: Temporary suspension or permanent deactivation of rider's account.

### Key Evidence
- Photo evidence with timestamp.
- Cleaning/repair receipt with company details.
- Trip timestamp.
- Rider's dispute evidence (if any).

## Scenario 7: Safety Incident (Harassment / Assault / Inappropriate Behavior)

### What Either Party Claims
A safety incident occurred during the trip — harassment, assault, inappropriate behavior, or reckless driving.

### Relevant Official Policies

**From Safety Standards — P0 Critical Safety Incidents:**
- Physical assault or threat of violence.
- Sexual harassment or misconduct.
- Reckless driving endangering life.
- Driving under the influence (alcohol or drugs).
- Stalking or following after trip completion.
- Hate speech or discrimination.

**Response:**
- Immediately escalated to human review.
- Involved driver's account may be temporarily suspended pending investigation.
- Criminal matters are reported to Singapore Police.

**From Code of Conduct:**
- Physical and verbal assault or harassment is strictly not tolerated.
- Drivers must not make remarks on race, religion, or other forms of personal beliefs.
- Contacting the passenger after the trip has ended is strictly prohibited.
- Passengers must avoid slurs or personal insults.

**From Terms of Use — Account Suspension:**
- Ryde may suspend or terminate accounts for safety incidents, fraudulent activity, or abusive behavior.
- Non-payment of verified cleaning fees may result in suspension or permanent deactivation.

**From Dispute Appeals Policy:**
- For immediate danger: Call Singapore Police at 999 (or 995 for medical emergency).
- Use the Ryde in-app emergency button to alert Ryde's safety team.
- Do not confront the other party.

### How to Resolve

1. **Immediate safety assessment**:
   - Is the reporter in immediate danger? If yes, advise calling 999/995.
   - Use in-app emergency button.
2. **Classify severity**:
   - **P0**: Physical assault, sexual harassment, reckless driving, DUI, stalking, hate speech.
   - **P1**: Verbal abuse, unsafe driving, refusal to follow safety instructions.
   - **P2/P3**: Inappropriate conversation, unprofessional appearance.
3. **Gather evidence**:
   - Incident description (what, when, where).
   - Booking/order ID.
   - Photos/screenshots.
   - Witness contact.
   - GPS telemetry (for reckless driving claims).
4. **Take action**:
   - **P0**: Immediate account suspension pending investigation. Report to police if criminal.
   - **P1**: Review within 24 hours. Formal warning. Pattern may escalate.
   - **P2/P3**: Document and monitor. No immediate action unless pattern.
5. **Support the victim**:
   - Offer resources (counseling, police support).
   - Ensure they feel safe using the platform.
   - Consider account protection measures.

### Key Evidence
- Incident report with detailed description.
- Booking/order ID.
- Photos/screenshots.
- GPS telemetry (speed, route, stops).
- In-app chat logs.
- Witness statements.
- Police report (if filed).

## General Dispute Resolution Principles

### 1. Evidence-Based Decisions
All disputes must be resolved based on evidence, not claims alone. Key evidence types:
- GPS telemetry (location, route, speed, timestamps).
- In-app chat logs.
- Payment and fare records.
- Photos with timestamps.
- Call logs.
- Ratings and reviews.

### 2. Proportionality
Consequences must be proportional to the severity of the violation:
- Minor issues: Warning, feedback, rating impact.
- Moderate issues: Temporary suspension, mandatory training.
- Severe issues: Permanent deactivation, police report.

### 3. Fairness to Both Parties
- Riders and drivers both have rights and obligations.
- Neither party is assumed to be at fault by default.
- Both parties can submit evidence and appeal decisions.

### 4. Ryde's Discretion
- Ryde retains absolute discretion over refunds and account actions.
- All decisions are subject to appeal via support@rydesharing.com.
- Partners may also use Singapore Mediation Centre or Small Claims Tribunal.

### 5. Timeline
- P0 incidents: Immediate response (human review within hours).
- P1 incidents: Review within 24 hours.
- Standard disputes: 3-5 business days.
- Refund processing: 3-5 business days after resolution.

### 6. Appeals
- Drivers submit appeals via RydeHELP in the driver app.
- Riders contact support@rydesharing.com.
- Ryde strives to resolve appeals within 3 days.
- Account restoration fee: S$10.00 (waived if appeal approved).
