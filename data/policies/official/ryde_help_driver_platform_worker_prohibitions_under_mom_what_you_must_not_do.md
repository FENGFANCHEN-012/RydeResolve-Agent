## What happens if I violate these rules?

Source: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/51618350667161-What-happens-if-I-violate-these-rules (last updated 2025-12-23)

- Violating these rules may result in serious consequences , including:
- Suspension or permanent deactivation of your Ryde account
- Loss of access to platform work and earnings
- Fines or enforcement action by authorities
- Possible action against your driving or vocational licence
- These measures are in place to protect compliant drivers and maintain a fair platform.
- MOM may investigate suspected violations.
- If found guilty under the Employment of Foreign Manpower Act, you will be debarred for a minimum of 24 months , even if the offence did not directly involve Ryde’s platform.
