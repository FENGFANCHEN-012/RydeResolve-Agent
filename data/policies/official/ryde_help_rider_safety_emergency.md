# Ryde Help Centre: Rider / Safety & Emergency

Official text from help.rydesharing.com, copied word for word (HTML removed). Fetched 2026-09-29.

## I was involved in an accident

Source: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/900003838306-I-was-involved-in-an-accident (last updated 2026-09-25)

Move quickly to a safe area away from traffic, if you and your driver are involved in an accident.
If you're in need of medical assistance, please call 995.
Exchange contact information with your driver.
You may file a medical claim through your driver’s commercial motor insurance.
Report to us swiftly via RydeHELP.

## Is there any insurance coverage in the event of an accident?

Source: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/115000777634-Is-there-any-insurance-coverage-in-the-event-of-an-accident (last updated 2025-10-24)

Carpool Drivers’ vehicle insurance remains valid as carpooling is not for profit. It is similar to giving friends or family a lift and then splitting trip costs.
Private Hire Drivers’ commercial insurance are valid for medical claims or compensation. Claims are to be filed through the driver.
Ryde does not provide any compensation or medical claims in the event of an accident.

## Report a safety issue

Source: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/51354502165913-Report-a-safety-issue (last updated 2026-09-25)

Seat belts are a mandatory requirement. Both you and your driver must wear them throughout the ride to ensure safety.
If the seat belt at your seat is damaged or if your driver isn't wearing one, let us know immediately. Your safety matters, and we’re here to ensure every ride is safe and comfortable.
How to report a seat belt issue
- To report a seat belt issue, follow these steps:
- Open the Ryde app and go to RydeHELP.
- Select Report a Safety Issue.
- Fill in the details of the issue, including the vehicle's registration number and any other relevant information.
- Submit the form, and our team will investigate promptly.
Your vigilance helps us maintain a safe and reliable experience for all. If you need any more help, please contact our support team via RydeHELP.
