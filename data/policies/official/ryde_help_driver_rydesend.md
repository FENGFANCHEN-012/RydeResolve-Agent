# Ryde Help Centre: Driver / RydeSEND

Official text from help.rydesharing.com, copied word for word (HTML removed). Fetched 2026-09-29.

## Can I request a refund of my RydeSEND deposit?

Source: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/360008005414-Can-I-request-a-refund-of-my-RydeSEND-deposit (last updated 2025-08-26)

Effective from 29 Dec 2021, Driver-partners can get their RydeSEND deposit refunded directly to their driver wallet within 30 days, if they wish to stop accepting RydeSEND trips. Kindly note that a $5 processing fee will still apply for the transaction.
Driver-partners can cash out their refund, along with earnings, at any time.

## Do I need to pay for damages or loss of items, how will Ryde charge me?

Source: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/360008119973-Do-I-need-to-pay-for-damages-or-loss-of-items-how-will-Ryde-charge-me (last updated 2025-08-26)

If the sender meets the conditions specified in our Terms of Use for a compensation claim, Ryde will deduct the compensation amount (up to $100) from your deposit. The final amount will be determined by Ryde.
You are solely responsible for any loss, damage, contamination, soiling, or detention related to the delivery item that occurs from the time of pickup until delivery and is caused directly by you.
Ryde has the complete and exclusive discretion to accept, deny, or determine a reasonable amount for final compensation.

## How are RydeSEND fares calculated?

Source: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/360008119853-How-are-RydeSEND-fares-calculated (last updated 2025-08-26)

Drivers will be paid according to the delivery charges shown in the app, subject to platform fees and service fees. These charges are based on distance travelled, time and location surcharge reflecting ERP and tolls.

## How do I cancel my RydeSEND trip?

Source: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/360008005454-How-do-I-cancel-my-RydeSEND-trip (last updated 2025-08-26)

We strongly discourage drivers from cancelling and encourage them to keep to their commitment. Contact senders in the event of a delivery request cancellation.
To cancel an accepted RydeSEND job:
-
Go to Trips on the main page.
- Tap on the upcoming delivery that you need to cancel
-
Tap CANCEL
-
Provide a reason for cancellation and tap CANCEL

## Report a RydeSEND Delivery Experience

Source: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/360008005514-Report-a-RydeSEND-Delivery-Experience (last updated 2025-08-26)

**There is no one to accept the item at the drop-off location.**
If an intended recipient is not contactable, please wait at the dropoff point for at least ten minutes and try to contact the recipient by SMS and phone.
Ryde encourages drivers to call recipients at least twice before contacting a user/sender to notify them of the absence of recipients.
If recipients are still not reachable, drivers are allowed to leave items at the dropoff point to complete the delivery unless users/senders and drivers agree on returning items to senders at users'/senders' cost. If users/senders want to request a return trip, Ryde recommends that users/senders pay additional fees to compensate for the return cost, which should be 75% of the delivery fees.
When rating users/senders, drivers can add a photo and notes as proof of delivery. Ryde recommends that drivers should take and upload a picture as proof of delivery to prevent a dispute.
**I cannot locate the sender's location to pick up the items.**
If the sender is not contactable, please wait at the pickup point for at least ten minutes and try to contact the sender by SMS and phone.
Ryde encourages drivers to call the sender to confirm the pick-up time and location prior to trip acceptance.
If you require more assistance, please share with us below.
**My recipient remains uncontactable**
You will be encouraged to call the receiver at least twice and wait for at least 10 minutes at the drop-off point to complete the delivery order. However, if the receiver is still uncontactable, drivers will be allowed to leave your items at the drop-off point at the sender's risk for any damages or loss.
Our customer excellence team will strive to respond to you within 2-3 working days.

## Why is there a $5 processing fee for the refund?

Source: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/50123912515865-Why-is-there-a-5-processing-fee-for-the-refund (last updated 2025-08-26)

The $5 processing fee covers the administrative and payment processing costs required to handle the refund securely and efficiently. The fee ensures your deposit is returned safely to your driver's wallet.
