# Ryde Help Centre: Rider / Booking a Ryde

Official text from help.rydesharing.com, copied word for word (HTML removed). Fetched 2026-09-29.

## Can I suggest my preferred route to the driver?

Source: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/4412121982745-Can-I-suggest-my-preferred-route-to-the-driver (last updated 2024-10-01)

The trip fare is fixed based on pick-up and drop-off points, hence the driver may decide to take the most efficient route according to GPS.
If you have a preferred route, you may recommend this to your driver and reach a mutual agreement before you go.

## I made a mess in my driver's vehicle

Source: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/900003466206-I-made-a-mess-in-my-driver-s-vehicle (last updated 2026-09-25)

Accidents can sometimes occur during a trip, leaving a mess that requires professional cleaning. While the vehicle is being cleaned, the driver is unable to continue taking trips.
In such cases, a cleaning fee may apply to cover the cleaning costs.
When you may be charged
If a mess during your trip requires professional cleaning, a cleaning fee may apply based on the severity of the incident. Fees vary by the type and severity of the mess, as shown below:

- Food, groceries, or drink spillage
- Sand or food crumbs

How is the cleaning fee determined?
Ryde reviews the submitted photos, trip details, and cleaning receipt to determine the appropriate amount, up to the applicable cap. For example, a verified vomit incident may result in a cleaning fee of up to S$200.
How do I pay the cleaning fee?
Our team will contact you to provide payment instructions once the contamination claim has been verified.
What happens if I do not pay the fee?
Your account may be temporarily placed on hold and you will not be able to book any new rides until the payment is cleared.
As set out in our Terms of Use , riders are responsible for professional cleaning fees where applicable
What if I did not cause the contamination?
If you believe a cleaning fee was incorrectly charged, please reach out to us via RydeHELP.

## If a driver arrives during the rider’s grace period, will  the grace period for cancellation be reduced?

Source: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/4407761270937-If-a-driver-arrives-during-the-rider-s-grace-period-will-the-grace-period-for-cancellation-be-reduced (last updated 2026-09-25)

Riders are guaranteed 3 minutes of grace period from the time of match and/or 15 minutes before scheduled time for Advance Trip Request before the Cancellation Fee kicks in.
