# Ryde Help Centre: Driver / Fares & Charges

Official text from help.rydesharing.com, copied word for word (HTML removed). Fetched 2026-09-29.

## How are the trip fares calculated?

Source: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/900005170043-How-are-the-trip-fares-calculated (last updated 2024-03-13)

Fares are calculated based on the distance covered between your pickup and drop off locations. Fares vary depending on the service type and are affected by surges.
The flag-down fare and regular booking fees inclusive of platform fees are applicable. The actual charges vary among different taxi companies. For more information on taxi fares, click here .

## How do I charge the Cancellation or Waiting Time Fee if the Rider pays by Cash?

Source: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408071485081-How-do-I-charge-the-Cancellation-or-Waiting-Time-Fee-if-the-Rider-pays-by-Cash (last updated 2023-06-25)

If the trip is cancelled by you or the rider according to the conditions in our Cancellation and Waiting Time Policy, the Cancellation Fee will be deducted from the rider's RydeCoins wallet and credited directly to your wallet.
If you have waited for the rider for more than 3 minutes and the trip has been completed with the Waiting Time Fee included, please tap on the Waiting Time Fee button upon drop-off, and collect the full fare as stated in the app.

## How Does the Cancellation Fee Work?

Source: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408070838297-How-Does-the-Cancellation-Fee-Work (last updated 2026-09-25)

Please let us know why you are cancelling.
Drivers will receive a $4.50 cancellation fee automatically if they cancel after >3 minutes of waiting.
Cancellations from no-shows will also be factored into the driver's account.

## How Does the Waiting Fee Work?

Source: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408071347993-How-Does-the-Waiting-Fee-Work (last updated 2026-09-25)

Drivers are required to tap on the $5* Waiting Time Fee button upon Drop-off (if applicable).

## How to add ERP to my trip fare?

Source: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/900005170163-How-to-add-ERP-to-my-trip-fare (last updated 2024-08-23)

How to input Toll (ERP) amount:
- Remember the amount of ERP incurred during the trip.
- Tap on Drop Off.
- Enter the amount in the box next to Toll (ERP).
- Rate your Rider.
- Input Reviews.
- Tap Confirm to end the trip.
Please bear in mind that if an additional / excessive amount is keyed in, this will be considered fraudulent and drivers will be liable for permanent suspension.

## What if I forgot to add in the Waiting Time Fee?

Source: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408079464217-What-if-I-forgot-to-add-in-the-Waiting-Time-Fee (last updated 2026-09-25)

Ryde will not be held accountable for the reimbursement of waiting time fees. Drivers are advised to tap on the waiting time fee toggle upon drop-off as we are unable to charge the rider once the trip is completed.

## What is Net Fare?

Source: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/52332111671833-What-is-Net-Fare (last updated 2025-11-28)

What is Net Fare?
It is the trip fare after deducting platform and service fees*.
*Only applicable for Carpool Drivers.
Why is the fare lower than before?
You are now seeing the Net Fare, your actual take-home amount, upfront instead of the gross fare. Your earnings have not changed.
Am I earning less now?
No change to your earnings or payout. Only the display is updated to reduce confusion.
What is Cash Collected?
This is only relevant for cash trips. It is the fare you will need to collect from your passenger. (Platform fee and Service fee will be deducted after the trip is completed from your wallet).
Why do riders see a different fare?
Riders see their total fare. Drivers see only their Net Fare (take-home).
Does this affect my bonuses?
No. Bonus calculation remains unchanged.
Can I still view gross fare?
You will be able to see the gross fare in your receipt after completing the trip.
What is "0% Service Fee Rebate"?
For PDVL/TDVL, we rebate your 10% service fee. This is now shown clearly in your receipt & wallet transaction.
Can I switch from Carpool to PDVL?
Yes, if eligible, upload your documents and our support team will verify your profile in 24hrs.
If you need help, visit RydeHELP and raise a ticket or contact our support team directly via Support@rydesharing.com.

## Which trips do I need to include ERP?

Source: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/115001438614-Which-trips-do-I-need-to-include-ERP (last updated 2026-09-02)

The fares for RydeSEND include ERP .
The fares for RydeFLASH / RydePET / RydeX / RydeXL / RydeLUXE / RydeTAXI exclude ERP .
Please key in ERP based on the ERP gantry you passed during the journey.
For Carpool trips:
- The total ERP charge is shared equally between the driver and passenger.
- The driver bears 50% of the ERP charge, and the passenger pays the remaining 50% .
Please bear in mind that if an additional/excessive amount is keyed in, this will be considered fraudulent and drivers will be liable for permanent suspension.
