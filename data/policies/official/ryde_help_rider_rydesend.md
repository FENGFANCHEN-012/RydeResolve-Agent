## Do I need to pay ERP charges and parking costs?

Source: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/360007924554-Do-I-need-to-pay-ERP-charges-and-parking-costs (last updated 2025-07-13)

Delivery fares are inclusive of tolls, ERP, and parking costs.

## Driver left my items at the door and my item was lost. Can I claim for compensation?

Source: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/360008031573-Driver-left-my-items-at-the-door-and-my-item-was-lost-Can-I-claim-for-compensation (last updated 2025-07-13)

We are sorry that your item was lost.
As we have specified in our Terms of Use , if a receiver is absent or uncontactable when the driver arrives at the drop-off point, drivers will be encouraged to call the receiver at least twice and wait for at least 10 minutes there to complete the delivery order.
However, if the receiver is still uncontactable, drivers will be allowed to leave your items at the drop-off point at the sender's risk for any damages or loss.
In such a case, we are not able to compensate for the loss of your items.

## How are the delivery fees calculated?

Source: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/360008031053-How-are-the-delivery-fees-calculated (last updated 2025-07-13)

Delivery fees are calculated based on distance, time, and additional delivery-related cost. Delivery fees are subject to surge as well.

We are sorry if you had a bad experience with your delivery. Please share your complaint or feedback with us through RydeHELP or by sending us an email at support@rydesharing.com .

-
Go to Trips .
-
Tap on Past .
- Select the trips that you want to feedback on.
-
Go to Help and select from the available topics.

We will do our best to respond to you within 24 hours.

## If my items suffered some damages or were lost during delivery, how can I claim for compensation?

Source: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/360008031513-If-my-items-suffered-some-damages-or-were-lost-during-delivery-how-can-I-claim-for-compensation (last updated 2025-07-13)

If you wish to make a claim for compensation, please email us at support@rydesharing.com with your order information and documented proof listed below within one day of the receipt date . Claims submitted after the stipulated time frame will not be entertained.
Ryde will investigate your case to determine if your case is applicable for compensation and assess the amounts of compensation.
For further information, please see our Terms of Use .
To make a claim for a lost parcel
- Please note that all second-hand items will not be applicable for compensation.
- Documented proof of value, which is a receipt or invoice showing the contents of your delivery items and their value.*
- Documented proof of loss, which is a signed and dated letter from the intended recipient confirming the item was never delivered.
- RydeSEND Claim Form
To make a claim for a damaged parcel
- Documented proof of value, which is a receipt or invoice showing the contents of your delivery items and their value.*
- Please note that all second-hand items will not be applicable for compensation.
- Photos clearly show your items and the damaged area.
- Photos of the items showing all the packaging that was used to protect your items during the delivery, including the area where the damage occurred.
- You shall ensure that all packaging is kept until the claim is resolved as we will need to see this during the investigation.
- RydeSEND Claim Form
*Required for a compensation claim
Please download the RydeSEND Claim Form below .

## When I cancel my order, will I be charged a cancellation fee?

Source: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/360007924474-When-I-cancel-my-order-will-I-be-charged-a-cancellation-fee (last updated 2025-07-13)

Yes, please note that cancellation charges may be applicable to senders, in accordance with our cancellation policy, in the event where the sender cancels the request from the moment the driver matches until the item is picked up.
You may refer to the Cancellation and Waiting Time policy for more details.
