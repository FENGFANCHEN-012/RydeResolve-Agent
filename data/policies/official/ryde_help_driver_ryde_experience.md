# Ryde Help Centre: Driver / Ryde Experience

Official text from help.rydesharing.com, copied word for word (HTML removed). Fetched 2026-09-29.

## Am I required to follow the suggested route by the rider?

Source: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/4412122016153-Am-I-required-to-follow-the-suggested-route-by-the-rider (last updated 2023-07-15)

The trip fare is fixed based on pick-up and drop-off points. So you may decide to take the fastest or most efficient route based on your GPS.
However, if you do not mind taking the route suggested by the rider, agreeing and travelling via the rider's suggested route may result in a happier ride!

## Fare Evasion

Source: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/4842763548825-Fare-Evasion (last updated 2026-09-25)

Please check the mode of payment before tapping on 'End Trip'. If his/her mode of payment is cash, please collect cash from the rider.
If the rider did not pay for the trip, kindly contact our support team at support@rydesharing.com with the title "Fare Evasion" and the details of the trip.
If you notice a rider has not paid their fare, please promptly file an online report with SPF regarding the fare evasion, and kindly submit the report to our team as well.
We will reimburse the fare to you as a gesture of goodwill.
Ryde will raise the issue to LTA for them to take the necessary actions.

## I would like to provide feedback about my rider

Source: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/20690825386521-I-would-like-to-provide-feedback-about-my-rider (last updated 2026-09-25)

** The rider was rude and/or made me feel unsafe. **
We feel your concern on such an experience. We are highly encouraging and expecting our riders to be respectful and polite, especially to our driver partners. As an open peer-to-peer platform, in order to better manage the community, members are able to provide ratings and honest reviews based on their experience.
Drivers do view riders'' ratings and reviews prior to confirming a trip. Our system automatically suspends drivers/riders who fall below a certain threshold. In the worst scenarios, riders will be permanently banned. To report the rider, send us a ticket via Rydehelp on your driver app.

## My Rider Made a Mess

Source: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/900004381243-My-Rider-Made-a-Mess (last updated 2026-09-25)

An unexpected mess shouldn't cost you your time on the road. When these incidents happen, Ryde is here to support you.
We understand that a mess costs you more than just a cleaning fee; it also takes you off the road. Ryde’s reimbursement tiers are structured to reflect both the cleaning cost and the downtime.
What should I do when there is a mess
- Riders are responsible for the cleaning expenses if they create a mess in your vehicle
- Do not demand compensation directly from riders; submit a claim to Ryde for the cleaning expenses to be reimbursed to you.
- Inform the passenger that the incident will be reported to Ryde
- At least one photo must show the vehicle’s car plate number to verify the vehicle
How to submit a claim:
- Your vehicle must be professionally cleaned within 24 hours from the incident
- Submit your claim via the driver app within 48 hours from the affected trip
- Provide the following details in your claim:
***Proof of mess (i.e. photo of the vomit, stains, etc.)*Receipt from the cleaning company with:**
- Company’s name, contact number, address
- Cleaning date
- Car plate number
- Authorised personnel's signature and company stamp
What kind of mess can I claim

All claims are subject to review. Ryde reserves the sole discretion to approve, deny, and decide on a fair reimbursement amount.
How and when will I get paid?
Once you submit your claim, our team will review the photos, cleaning receipt, and may verify the incident details where required.
If your claim is approved, your reimbursement will be credited to you promptly after approval. The reimbursement is guaranteed and is not dependent on the rider completing payment, as Ryde will manage the recovery process separately.
You can appeal within 24 hours if you disagree with the outcome of your claim with any additional supporting evidence.
What if the cleaning cost exceeds the policy cap?
Reimbursement will be capped based on the applicable tier (up to S$200). Any excess amount will not be covered.
Important
*Mess that can be cleaned immediately without professional cleaning will not be reimbursed.
*Fur and saliva from pets are expected during pet-friendly trips and will not be reimbursed.

## What if my Rider rates me poorly because of the Cancellation or Waiting Time Fee?

Source: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408079364889-What-if-my-Rider-rates-me-poorly-because-of-the-Cancellation-or-Waiting-Time-Fee (last updated 2026-09-25)

If you feel that you have been unfairly rated due to the additional fees imposed on riders, please write in using the RydeHELP feature on your app to submit your appeal. Based on our Ratings and Review Moderation Policy, the team will review your appeal and consider removing ratings that are irrelevant to the driver's quality of service.
