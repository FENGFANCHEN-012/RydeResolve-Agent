## Understanding the Base Fare Components

Source: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/46374495669401-Understanding-the-Base-Fare-Components (last updated 2025-05-15)

Ryde Carpool operates under the Road Traffic (Car Pools) (Exemption) Order 2021 and the LTA Point-to-Point (P2P) regulatory framework , which regulate carpooling in Singapore as a not-for-profit activity. This means that carpool drivers may recover reasonable costs such as fuel, tolls, and vehicle maintenance, but are not permitted to make a profit from these trips.
To support the sustainability of the carpool ecosystem, a portion of the total fare is retained by Ryde to cover:
- Technology infrastructure and platform maintenance
- Customer support services
- Safety and trust systems
- Incentives and bonuses
- Regulatory compliance efforts
These deductions enable Ryde to continue operating a safe, reliable, and compliant carpooling platform for both drivers and riders.
Note: Carpool drivers are limited to two paid trips per day , in accordance with LTA regulations.
