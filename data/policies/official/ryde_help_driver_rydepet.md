# Ryde Help Centre: Driver / RydePET

Official text from help.rydesharing.com, copied word for word (HTML removed). Fetched 2026-09-29.

## Can I cancel the trip if I don't want to carry a particular pet, especially once the rider is about to board?

Source: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/49717696369561-Can-I-cancel-the-trip-if-I-don-t-want-to-carry-a-particular-pet-especially-once-the-rider-is-about-to-board (last updated 2026-09-25)

We strongly discourage last-minute cancellations, as they can disrupt the rider's experience and may impact your driver rating.
To help you make an informed decision, pet details are shared upfront on the job card and in the ping - including the pet type and whether it will be in a carrier or on a leash.
If you are not comfortable with a particular pet arrangement, we kindly ask that you decline the job before accepting it. Once a job has been accepted, we appreciate your commitment to completing the ride for the rider.
Can I cancel the trip if the rider chose the wrong category?
Yes, you may cancel the booking if the rider has selected an incorrect service for their pet. For example, booking a standard ride with a pet, or choosing “Carrier | Dog” when “Leashed | Dog” would be more appropriate for their setup.
Please select "Other" as your cancellation reason. This standardised tagging helps us track pet mismatches consistently. Include a brief note with details if needed.
Please note that all cancellations remain subject to Ryde’s cancellation policy.

## What happens if I cancel after accepting due to the pet?

Source: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/49718401889561-What-happens-if-I-cancel-after-accepting-due-to-the-pet (last updated 2026-09-25)

You are encouraged to honour trips once accepted. Cancelling because of the pet type may lead to a review of the situation and possible penalties to ensure fairness and reliability for all users.
