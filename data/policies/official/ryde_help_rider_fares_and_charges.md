## Cancellation and Waiting Fee

Source: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/23534478435353-Cancellation-and-Waiting-Fee (last updated 2026-02-26)

On-demand Trip - Rider Cancellation & Waiting Time Policy Statement
All cancellations made within 3 minutes of matching are not chargeable.
Rider will be charged a Cancellation Fee of $6.61* if:
- Rider cancels after 3 minutes of matching OR
- The driver presses “I’m Here”, waits for > 3 minutes for the rider to arrive at the pick-up point and cancels the trip (Rider NO SHOW and no trip completion)
Riders may be charged a Waiting Time Fee of $5 at the end of the trip if:
- The driver presses “I’m Here”, waits for > 3 minutes for the rider to board, and continues the trip (Rider boards and driver completes trip).
Advanced Trip - Rider Cancellation & Waiting Time Policy Statement
All cancellations made > 15 minutes before the scheduled trip time are not chargeable.
(eg: Scheduled trip: 6 PM, any cancellation made before 5:45 PM is not chargeable.
If the advanced trip request is accepted only after 5:45 PM, riders will have a 3 minute grace period )
Rider will be charged a Cancellation Fee of $6.61* if:
- Rider cancels after the cancellation window (< Scheduled - 15mins OR Matched) OR
- The driver cancels the trip after waiting for > 3 minutes for the Rider to arrive at the pick-up point (Rider NO SHOW and no trip completion)
Riders may be charged a Waiting Time Fee of $5 at the end of the trip if:
- The driver waits for > 3 minutes for the Rider to board and continues to complete the trip (Rider boards and driver completes trip)
* For fares up to $18, a flat fee of $1.15 (exclusive of GST) & for fares exceeding $18, a flat fee of $1.35 (exclusive of GST) will be included in the base fare. Cashless transactions will incur a payment transaction fee of 1.96%+ $0.24 (inclusive of GST) will apply for cashless transactions.

## Cancellation Fee Waiver Request

Source: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/18229758389017-Cancellation-Fee-Waiver-Request (last updated 2026-09-25)

If you feel that you have been unfairly charged a Cancellation Fee, you may submit a fee waiver request via RydeHELP in our app within 30 days of the trip* and we will review your eligibility for a refund.
If you are eligible, we will issue you a voucher** with a unique code worth $6.61 as a refund for the incurred Cancellation Charges. Please be mindful to top up your Ryde wallet using the voucher code given to you within the expiry period.
*Requests submitted after 30 days of the trip will not be considered for waivers.
**This voucher is non-refundable and cannot be exchanged for cash in part or in full. There is strictly no extension of the expiry date from the date of issue.
The increase is due to the platform fee changes effective 17 Feb 2026.

## Do I need to pay ERP charges?

Source: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/115000772653-Do-I-need-to-pay-ERP-charges (last updated 2025-11-23)

If a rider passes through an active ERP gantry, the toll charge will be applicable to the rider.
For all services apart from RydeSEND , ERP charges are excluded from the fares.
* RydeSEND fares include ERP charges.

## Does the Cancellation Policy apply to all Ryde services?

Source: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/4407761313305-Does-the-Cancellation-Policy-apply-to-all-Ryde-services (last updated 2025-11-23)

Cancellation Policy applies to all Service Types * in the Ryde app and is executed on a service level, not driver level. Hence, all carpool drivers are included in the Cancellation Policy.
*All services except RydeTAXI

## How am I charged under the Policy if I am paying my fare by Cash?

Source: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408070775193-How-am-I-charged-under-the-Policy-if-I-am-paying-my-fare-by-Cash (last updated 2025-11-23)

If the trip is cancelled by the rider or the driver according to the conditions in our Cancellation and Waiting Time Policy, the Cancellation or Waiting Time Fee will be deducted from your RydeCoins wallet and credited directly to the driver's wallet.
If the driver has waited for you for more than 3 minutes and the trip has been completed with the Waiting Time Fee included, please pay the driver according to the final calculated fare stated on the app.

## How is the Payment Transaction Fee calculated?

Source: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/52596204611993-How-is-the-Payment-Transaction-Fee-calculated (last updated 2025-11-23)

The fee is based on card type and will be shown as “Transaction Fee” on your ride receipt.

- 3.3% + $0.50 for AMEX, Foreign Cards & Others

## Understanding the Fare Structure

Source: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/46946509938073-Understanding-the-Fare-Structure (last updated 2025-11-23)

1. Trip Fare
This is the core charge based on the trip’s distance and duration, ensuring that the cost aligns with the ride you take.
2. Service Fee & Platform Fee
Supports day-to-day platform operations and fuels ongoing innovation to improve both the driver and passenger experience.
3. Other Fees & Taxes
This includes GST, transaction fees, and any applicable taxes or charges.
The rider’s fare ultimately supports a wide range of services, from driver earnings to platform sustainability. After deducting all other applicable fees and charges, Ryde reinvests its retained share into enhancing product features, improving service reliability, bonuses & incentives, and supporting long-term growth initiatives.

## Why am I charged for a Waiting Time Fee?

Source: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/4407754351257-Why-am-I-charged-for-a-Waiting-Time-Fee (last updated 2025-11-23)

A Waiting Time Fee allows our Driver-Partners to be compensated by riders for the time spent waiting at a pickup location.
Waiting Time Fees begin to kick in after the driver waits for more than 3 minutes at the pick-up point for the rider to board.

## Will I be charged for cancelling a ride?

Source: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/4407761228825-Will-I-be-charged-for-cancelling-a-ride (last updated 2025-11-23)

Cancellation Fees compensate drivers for the time and effort they spend getting to your location.
You can cancel a trip at any time through the Ryde app, but may be charged a Cancellation Fee if you cancel after 3 minutes of matching with a driver.
