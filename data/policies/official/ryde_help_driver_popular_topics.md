## Cancellation and Waiting Time Policy

Source: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/20244064619161-Cancellation-and-Waiting-Time-Policy (last updated 2024-12-19)

** On-Demand Trip **
A Cancellation Fee compensates drivers for the time and effort they spend getting to the rider’s location.
A Waiting Time Fee allows our drivers to be compensated by riders for the time spent waiting at a pickup location.
All cancellations made within 3 minutes of matching are not chargeable.
Driver will receive a Cancellation Fee of $4.50* if:
- Rider cancels after 3 minutes of matching
Driver may charge a Waiting Time Fee of $4.50 at the end of the trip if:
-
The driver pressed “I’m Here”, waits for > 3 minutes for rider to arrive at pick-up point and cancels the trip (Rider NO SHOW and no trip completion), OR
- The driver pressed “I’m Here”, waits for > 3 minutes for rider to board, and continues the trip (Rider boards and driver completes trip).
** Advanced Trip **
All cancellations made more than 15 minutes before scheduled trip time are not chargeable.
For example:
If the scheduled trip is at 6PM, any cancellation made before 5:45PM is not chargeable.
If the advanced trip request is accepted only after 5:45PM, riders will have a 3 minutes grace period.
Driver will receive a Cancellation Fee of $4.50 if:
- Rider cancels after the cancellation window (< Scheduled - 15mins OR Matched).
Driver may charge a Waiting Time Fee of $4.50 at the end of the trip if:
-
The driver pressed “I’m Here”, waits for > 3 minutes for rider to arrive at the pick-up point and cancels the trip (Rider NO SHOW and no trip completion), OR
- The driver pressed “I’m Here”, waits for > 3 minutes for rider to board, and continues the trip (Rider boards and driver completes trip).
This Policy applies for all Service Types **.
**Please refer to the Ryde app for the comprehensive list of service options.

## I wish to make an appeal

Source: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/20244103989529-I-wish-to-make-an-appeal (last updated 2026-09-25)

Request for cancellation & waiting fee
To qualify for a cancellation/waiting fee, the following conditions must be met:
You arrived at the pickup location and the customer was not present within 3 minutes of your arrival.
In the event that a customer cancels the booking, the fee will be automatically credited to you, provided you meet the eligibility criteria.
Appealing for Cancellation/Waiting Fee Due to Various Reasons
1. Rider booked the wrong service or wrong location and refused to cancel
2. Rider cancelled the trip once you reached the pickup point
3. Rider came with additional riders
4. Rider arrived with a child without a booster seat
5. My RydeSEND order was delayed for more than 3 minutes and I did not receive the waiting fee
6. My RydeSEND order was cancelled after 3 minutes and I did not receive the cancellation fee
7. I cancelled the RydeSEND trip as the order was not ready after 3 minutes but did not receive the cancellation fee
If you forgot to include ERP charges in the fares
You may file a claim with the following details:
1. Name of ERP Gantry
2. Time of Entry
3. Screenshot of Cashcard Statement
Note: RydePOOL, RydeHIRE and RydeSEND include ERP.
My rider made a mess in my vehicle
1. Vomit
2. Seat stains
3. Spillage
4. Soil
Tip: Keeping Plastic Bags Handy & newspaper to protect Your Seats with Pets
Having plastic bags easily accessible in your car can be helpful if any passenger feels nauseous or needs to vomit. Consider using newspapers to keep your seats clean if you allow pets in your vehicle.
Please let us know via the RydeHELP feature on your app and include the following details:
- Photo evidence (e.g. a picture of the vomit/stain/etc)
- Receipt from the Cleaning Company with the following details:
- Name, contact number & address
- Date of cleaning
- Authorised personnel’s signature/company stamp

## Ratings and Reviews Moderation Policy

Source: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/20244161586585-Ratings-and-Reviews-Moderation-Policy (last updated 2024-12-19)

At Ryde, our Driver Rating system is in place to maintain high Driver-Partner quality. However, we understand that sometimes ratings and reviews may have been unfairly given to you by riders.
As such, rest assured that a review may be removed and ratings be reinstated if:
- The review is in violation of our Terms of Use ,
- The review is biased or fraudulent;
- The review is irrelevant to the rider's Point-to-Point transport experience
Conflict of interest
- We will remove reviews where there are signals that the trip request was made for the sole purpose of inflating a person’s overall rating, or where we suspect that the trip never occurred i.e. a fake-complete trip.
- Accepting fake requests in exchange for a positive review, using a second account to review your driving performance, or providing something of value in exchange for positive reviews, are not allowed. Violations may result in the restriction, suspension, or termination of your Ryde driver account.
Moderating for relevance
Reviews and its derived ratings that are irrelevant to a driver’s performance can be distracting and impact the driver’s overall rating. For this reason, rider reviews should focus on your driving service during the trip experience. To keep reviews relevant, we recommend avoiding the following:
- Commentary about a person’s social, political, or religious views;
- Profanity, name-calling, and assumptions about a person’s character or personality;
- Content that refers to circumstances entirely outside of the driver’s control (e.g. Mapping / GPS / Product / Technical Issues);
- Content about services not related to Ryde (e.g. Ryde’s competitors);
- Commentary about past trips or drivers or about the Ryde product where it does not relate to the driver and the trip you are rating (Cashbacks, app UI/UX, Waiting Time Fee etc).
When we receive a report of a review that violates this policy, we may remove the review from our platform and reinstate your 5* rating. Repeated violations may lead to suspension or permanent deactivation of the account(s) responsible for the reviews.
